## Tips for Using These Resources

1. **Start with NIELIT's official syllabus** — know exactly what topics are in the exam
2. **Watch YouTube videos** for topics you find difficult — visual explanations help
3. **Take at least 5 mock tests** before the exam — practice the 100 MCQ, 90-minute format
4. **Use the number converter tool** to practice binary-decimal conversions until they become easy
5. **Focus on full forms and definitions** — the CCC exam asks many direct factual questions
6. **Revise the cheatsheet** (provided separately) the night before the exam
