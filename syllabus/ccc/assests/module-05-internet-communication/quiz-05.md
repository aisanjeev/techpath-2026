# Quiz: Computer Communication and Internet

**Module 05 — CCC Exam Preparation**

---

### Q1. LAN (Local Area Network) typically covers which area?

- A) An entire country
- B) A single building or office ✅
- C) A city or metropolitan area
- D) The entire world

> **Explanation:** LAN (Local Area Network) covers a small area such as a single building, office, school lab, or campus. It is the most common type of network you use daily.

---

### Q2. What is the full form of DNS?

- A) Data Network System
- B) Domain Name System ✅
- C) Digital Network Service
- D) Direct Name Server

> **Explanation:** DNS stands for Domain Name System. It converts human-readable domain names (like google.com) into IP addresses (like 142.250.77.110) that computers understand.

---

### Q3. What does a modem convert?

- A) AC current to DC current
- B) Digital signals to analog signals and vice versa ✅
- C) Text to images
- D) Sound to light signals

> **Explanation:** A modem (Modulator-Demodulator) converts digital signals from your computer into analog signals for transmission over phone lines, and converts incoming analog signals back to digital.

---

### Q4. Which protocol is used to send emails?

- A) HTTP
- B) FTP
- C) SMTP ✅
- D) POP3

> **Explanation:** SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol) is used to send emails. POP3 and IMAP are used to receive emails. Remember: SMTP = Sending Mail To People.

---

### Q5. What is the full form of ISP?

- A) Internet Service Protocol
- B) Internet Service Provider ✅
- C) Internal System Protocol
- D) International Service Provider

> **Explanation:** ISP stands for Internet Service Provider. It is a company (like Jio, Airtel, BSNL) that provides you access to the internet.

---

### Q6. What is the largest network in the world?

- A) LAN
- B) MAN
- C) The Internet ✅
- D) ARPANET

> **Explanation:** The Internet is the world's largest network. It is a global WAN (Wide Area Network) that connects billions of computers and devices worldwide.

---

### Q7. What does UMANG stand for?

- A) United Mobile Application for National Governance
- B) Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance ✅
- C) Universal Mobile App for Next Generation
- D) Unified Management Application for Government

> **Explanation:** UMANG stands for Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance. It is a single app that provides access to multiple government services like Aadhaar, PAN, passport, EPFO, and more.

---

### Q8. In Star topology, what happens when the central hub fails?

- A) Only one computer is affected
- B) The entire network goes down ✅
- C) The network switches to Ring topology
- D) Nothing happens, network continues working

> **Explanation:** In Star topology, all devices connect to a central hub or switch. If this central device fails, the entire network goes down because all communication passes through it.

---

### Q9. Which year was ARPANET — the ancestor of the Internet — created?

- A) 1959
- B) 1969 ✅
- C) 1979
- D) 1989

> **Explanation:** ARPANET was created in 1969 by the US Department of Defense. It initially connected 4 universities and is considered the ancestor of today's Internet.

---

### Q10. Who invented the World Wide Web (WWW)?

- A) Bill Gates
- B) Steve Jobs
- C) Tim Berners-Lee ✅
- D) Mark Zuckerberg

> **Explanation:** Tim Berners-Lee invented the World Wide Web (WWW) in 1989 at CERN, Switzerland. Note: WWW and Internet are not the same — Internet is the network, WWW is a service running on it.

---

### Q11. What is the maximum speed of a dial-up internet connection?

- A) 56 Kbps ✅
- B) 1 Mbps
- C) 10 Mbps
- D) 100 Mbps

> **Explanation:** Dial-up connection has a maximum speed of 56 Kbps (kilobits per second), making it the slowest type of internet connection. It uses a regular telephone line and you cannot use the phone while connected.

---

### Q12. Fiber optic cables transmit data using which medium?

- A) Electrical signals through copper wire
- B) Radio waves through air
- C) Light signals through glass fibers ✅
- D) Sound waves through cable

> **Explanation:** Fiber optic cables use thin glass or plastic fibers to transmit data as pulses of light. This makes fiber optic the fastest and most reliable type of internet connection.

---

### Q13. A firewall is used to:

- A) Speed up the internet connection
- B) Block unauthorized access and filter network traffic ✅
- C) Convert digital signals to analog
- D) Store files on the cloud

> **Explanation:** A firewall is a security system that monitors and controls incoming and outgoing network traffic. It acts as a barrier between your trusted network and the untrusted internet, blocking unauthorized access.

---

### Q14. What is the difference between a virus and a worm?

- A) A virus is harmful but a worm is not
- B) A virus needs a host file to spread; a worm spreads on its own ✅
- C) A worm is faster than a virus
- D) There is no difference

> **Explanation:** A computer virus attaches itself to a host file and spreads when that file is opened or shared. A worm can replicate and spread on its own through networks without needing a host file.

---

### Q15. DigiLocker is used for:

- A) Making online payments
- B) Video conferencing
- C) Storing government documents digitally in the cloud ✅
- D) Sending emails to government offices

> **Explanation:** DigiLocker is a Digital India initiative that provides a cloud-based platform to store and share official documents like Aadhaar, PAN card, driving license, and mark sheets digitally.
