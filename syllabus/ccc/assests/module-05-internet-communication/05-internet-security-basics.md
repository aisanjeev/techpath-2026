## Why Internet Security Matters

The internet is a wonderful tool, but it also has dangers. Just like you lock your home to keep it safe, you need to protect your computer and personal information on the internet.

Every day, lakhs of Indians fall victim to online fraud, fake websites, and data theft. Understanding internet security helps you stay safe online and is an important topic in the CCC exam.

## Common Internet Threats

### 1. Virus

A **computer virus** is a harmful program that can copy itself and spread from one computer to another. It attaches itself to legitimate files or programs and activates when you open them.

**What a virus can do:**
- Delete or corrupt your files
- Slow down your computer
- Steal your personal information
- Crash your operating system

**How viruses spread:**
- Through email attachments
- Through infected pen drives
- Through downloading software from untrusted websites
- Through infected websites

**CCC Exam Tip:** A computer virus is a program that can replicate (copy itself) and spread to other computers. It needs a host file to attach to.

### 2. Malware

**Malware** (Malicious Software) is a general term for any harmful software. Virus is one type of malware, but there are others:

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Virus** | Attaches to files, spreads when file is opened |
| **Worm** | Spreads on its own without needing a host file |
| **Trojan Horse** | Disguises as useful software but is actually harmful |
| **Spyware** | Secretly monitors your activity and steals data |
| **Ransomware** | Locks your files and demands money (ransom) to unlock them |
| **Adware** | Shows unwanted advertisements on your computer |
| **Keylogger** | Records every key you press (to steal passwords) |

**Real-life example:** In 2017, the WannaCry ransomware attacked computers worldwide, including some in India. It locked users' files and demanded payment in Bitcoin to unlock them.

**CCC Exam Tip:** Know the difference between virus, worm, and trojan. Virus needs a host file; worm spreads on its own; trojan pretends to be useful software.

### 3. Phishing

**Phishing** is a type of online fraud where criminals try to trick you into revealing personal information (like passwords, bank details, or Aadhaar number) by pretending to be a trusted organization.

**How phishing works:**
1. You receive an email or SMS that looks like it is from your bank (e.g., SBI, HDFC)
2. The message says: "Your account will be blocked. Click here to verify your details."
3. You click the link and see a website that looks exactly like your bank's website
4. You enter your username, password, and OTP
5. The criminals now have your login details and can steal your money

**How to identify phishing:**
- Check the sender's email address carefully (e.g., `support@sbi-secure-login.com` is FAKE — real SBI email would be from `@sbi.co.in`)
- Look for spelling mistakes and poor grammar
- Banks never ask for passwords, PINs, or OTPs via email or SMS
- Check the URL — phishing sites often have slightly different URLs

**CCC Exam Tip:** Phishing is a social engineering attack where criminals pretend to be a trusted organization to steal personal information.

### 4. Spam

**Spam** is unwanted, unsolicited messages sent in bulk — usually through email.

- Spam fills up your inbox with junk mail
- Some spam contains phishing links or malware attachments
- Email services (like Gmail) have spam filters that automatically detect and block spam

**Example:** Priya receives 50 emails about "Win a free iPhone!" or "You have won Rs. 10 lakh lottery!" — these are all spam.

## Protection Against Internet Threats

### 1. Firewall

A **firewall** is a security system that monitors and controls incoming and outgoing network traffic. It acts as a barrier between your trusted internal network and the untrusted internet.

**How a firewall works:**
```
Internet  ←→  [FIREWALL]  ←→  Your Computer
                  ↓
          Blocks harmful traffic
          Allows safe traffic
```

**Types of firewalls:**
- **Software Firewall:** A program installed on your computer (e.g., Windows Firewall)
- **Hardware Firewall:** A physical device between your network and the internet (used in offices)

**CCC Exam Tip:** A firewall acts as a barrier between your computer and the internet. It blocks unauthorized access while allowing legitimate communication.

### 2. Antivirus Software

**Antivirus software** detects, prevents, and removes malware from your computer.

**Popular antivirus software:**
| Software | Type |
|----------|------|
| **Windows Defender** | Free, built into Windows 10/11 |
| **Quick Heal** | Indian company, popular in India |
| **Kaspersky** | International, well-known |
| **Norton** | International, comprehensive protection |
| **Avast** | Free version available |

**Best practices:**
- Keep your antivirus updated
- Run regular full scans
- Do not disable the antivirus
- Windows Defender is sufficient for most users

### 3. Strong Passwords

A **strong password** protects your accounts from being hacked. Many people use weak passwords like "123456" or "password" — these can be guessed in seconds.

**Rules for strong passwords:**
- At least 8 characters long (12+ is better)
- Mix of uppercase letters (A-Z), lowercase letters (a-z), numbers (0-9), and special characters (!@#$)
- Do not use personal information (name, birthday, phone number)
- Do not use the same password for all accounts
- Change passwords regularly

**Examples:**
| Weak Password | Strong Password |
|---------------|-----------------|
| 123456 | T3chP@th2026! |
| password | Bh0p@l_R@in#42 |
| rahul123 | M@ng0_Tr33$2025 |
| india | $n3h@_D3lh!99 |

**CCC Exam Tip:** A strong password should have a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters. Minimum 8 characters.

### 4. Safe Browsing Practices

Follow these rules to stay safe while browsing the internet:

1. **Check for HTTPS** — Always look for the padlock icon and "https://" in the URL before entering sensitive information
2. **Do not click unknown links** — Especially in emails and SMS from unknown senders
3. **Verify websites** — Before entering bank details, check the URL carefully
4. **Keep software updated** — Install Windows updates and browser updates regularly
5. **Use official app stores** — Download apps only from Google Play Store or Apple App Store
6. **Be careful on public WiFi** — Do not access bank accounts on free public WiFi
7. **Log out after use** — Always log out of accounts on shared computers
8. **Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** — Adds an extra layer of security (OTP on phone)

## Digital India Initiatives

The **Digital India** programme was launched by the Government of India on **1 July 2015** to transform India into a digitally empowered society.

### Key Digital India Services:

### 1. DigiLocker
- A cloud-based platform to store and share official documents digitally
- Stores documents like Aadhaar card, PAN card, driving license, mark sheets
- Accepted as valid documents by government agencies
- Website: digilocker.gov.in
- No need to carry physical documents

### 2. UMANG App
- **UMANG** stands for **Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance**
- One app that provides access to multiple government services
- Services include: Aadhaar, PAN, passport, EPFO, gas booking, electricity bill, exam results
- Available on Android and iOS

### 3. e-Governance

**e-Governance** means providing government services to citizens through electronic means (internet).

**Important e-Governance services:**

| Service | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Aadhaar (UIDAI)** | Unique 12-digit identity number for every Indian |
| **Income Tax e-Filing** | File income tax returns online |
| **Passport Seva** | Apply for passport online |
| **IRCTC** | Book train tickets online |
| **UPI** | Instant mobile payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) |
| **Parivahan** | Vehicle registration, driving license services |
| **UMANG** | Single app for multiple government services |
| **DigiLocker** | Store government documents digitally |
| **e-Hospital** | Online appointment booking for government hospitals |
| **PM Kisan** | Direct benefit transfer for farmers |
| **Common Service Centers (CSC)** | Internet access points in rural areas |

**CCC Exam Tip:** "UMANG stands for?" = Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance. "DigiLocker is used for?" = Storing government documents digitally. "When was Digital India launched?" = 1 July 2015.

## Cyber Crime and Cyber Law

### What is Cyber Crime?
Any criminal activity that involves a computer and a network is called **cyber crime**.

**Types of cyber crimes:**
- Hacking — Unauthorized access to computer systems
- Identity theft — Stealing someone's personal information
- Online fraud — Fake websites, lottery scams
- Cyberbullying — Harassing someone online
- Data theft — Stealing confidential data

### IT Act, 2000
- India's primary law for dealing with cyber crime
- **Information Technology Act, 2000** (IT Act) provides legal recognition for electronic transactions
- Amended in 2008 to cover more types of cyber crimes
- Establishes **Cyber Appellate Tribunal** to handle disputes

### Where to Report Cyber Crime in India:
- **National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal:** cybercrime.gov.in
- **Helpline:** 1930 (for financial fraud)
- Local police station (cyber cell)
