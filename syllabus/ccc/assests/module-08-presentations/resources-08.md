## Quick Reference

16. **PowerPoint Keyboard Shortcuts (Complete List)**
    - https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/office/use-keyboard-shortcuts-to-create-powerpoint-presentations
    - Official Microsoft keyboard shortcuts reference

17. **Key Shortcuts for CCC Exam**
    - F5 = Slide show from beginning
    - Shift + F5 = Slide show from current slide
    - Esc = End slide show
    - Ctrl + M = New slide
    - Ctrl + D = Duplicate slide
    - Ctrl + P = Print

## Recommended Study Plan

| Day | Topic | Time |
|-----|-------|------|
| Day 1 | PowerPoint interface, views (Normal, Slide Sorter, Slide Show) | 45 min |
| Day 2 | Creating slides, layouts, Slide Master, templates | 45 min |
| Day 3 | Inserting objects (tables, pictures, SmartArt, WordArt, shapes) | 45 min |
| Day 4 | Themes, design, fonts, backgrounds, headers and footers | 45 min |
| Day 5 | Transitions vs Animations — types, applying, timing | 60 min |
| Day 6 | Slide Show (F5/Shift+F5), Presenter View, Kiosk mode, Rehearse Timings | 60 min |
| Day 7 | Printing (handouts) and exporting (PDF, video) | 30 min |
| Day 8 | Cheatsheet revision + Quiz practice | 45 min |
| Day 9 | Mock test — PowerPoint chapter only | 30 min |
| Day 10 | Revise weak areas based on mock test results | 30 min |

**Note:** This module has the third highest exam weight (14-16 questions). Spend extra time on views, F5/Shift+F5, transitions vs animations, and handout printing.
