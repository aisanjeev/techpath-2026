# Quiz: Communication & Collaboration — Email

**Module 07 — CCC Exam Preparation**

---

### Q1. What does CC stand for in email?

- A) Computer Copy
- B) Carbon Copy ✅
- C) Certified Copy
- D) Central Copy

> **Explanation:** CC stands for Carbon Copy. It is used to send a copy of the email to additional recipients, and all recipients can see who is in the CC field.

---

### Q2. BCC recipients in an email are visible to ___.

- A) All recipients
- B) Only CC recipients
- C) Only the sender
- D) No one except the sender ✅

> **Explanation:** BCC stands for Blind Carbon Copy. BCC recipients are hidden from all other recipients — only the sender knows who was added in the BCC field.

---

### Q3. SMTP is used to ___.

- A) Receive email
- B) Send email ✅
- C) Store email
- D) Delete email

> **Explanation:** SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol) is the protocol used for sending emails from a client to a server and between mail servers.

---

### Q4. POP3 is used to ___.

- A) Send email
- B) Compose email
- C) Download/receive email ✅
- D) Format email

> **Explanation:** POP3 (Post Office Protocol version 3) is used to download and receive emails from the mail server to your local device.

---

### Q5. What is the maximum attachment size in Gmail?

- A) 10 MB
- B) 15 MB
- C) 20 MB
- D) 25 MB ✅

> **Explanation:** Gmail allows attachments up to 25 MB in size. If the file is larger than 25 MB, Gmail automatically uses Google Drive to share the file.

---

### Q6. Emails that have been composed but not yet sent are stored in the ___ folder.

- A) Inbox
- B) Sent
- C) Drafts ✅
- D) Outbox

> **Explanation:** Unsent or incomplete emails are automatically saved in the Drafts folder. You can open them later to continue editing and send.

---

### Q7. The @ symbol in an email address separates ___.

- A) First name and last name
- B) Username and password
- C) Username and domain name ✅
- D) Domain and country code

> **Explanation:** The @ (at) symbol separates the username (local part) from the domain name in an email address. For example, in rahul@gmail.com, 'rahul' is the username and 'gmail.com' is the domain.

---

### Q8. Which protocol keeps emails on the server and syncs them across multiple devices?

- A) SMTP
- B) POP3
- C) IMAP ✅
- D) FTP

> **Explanation:** IMAP (Internet Message Access Protocol) keeps emails stored on the server and synchronizes them across all devices. This is why the same emails appear on your phone, laptop, and tablet.

---

### Q9. Typing in ALL CAPITALS on the internet is considered as ___.

- A) Whispering
- B) Shouting ✅
- C) Formal writing
- D) Proper netiquette

> **Explanation:** In netiquette (internet etiquette), typing in ALL CAPS is considered as SHOUTING. It is considered rude and should be avoided in emails and online messages.

---

### Q10. What is the full form of SMTP?

- A) Simple Message Transfer Protocol
- B) Simple Mail Transfer Protocol ✅
- C) Secure Mail Transfer Protocol
- D) Standard Mail Transfer Protocol

> **Explanation:** SMTP stands for Simple Mail Transfer Protocol. It is the standard protocol used for sending emails across the internet.

---

### Q11. Sent emails are automatically stored in the ___ folder.

- A) Inbox
- B) Outbox
- C) Sent Mail ✅
- D) Drafts

> **Explanation:** When you send an email, a copy is automatically saved in the Sent Mail (or Sent) folder. This serves as a record of all emails you have sent.

---

### Q12. What is phishing?

- A) A type of computer virus
- B) Sending bulk advertising emails
- C) Fake emails designed to steal personal information ✅
- D) A method to block spam

> **Explanation:** Phishing is a cyber attack where criminals send fake emails pretending to be from trusted organizations (like banks) to trick recipients into revealing personal information such as passwords, PINs, or bank details.

---

### Q13. In Gmail, deleted emails are permanently removed after ___ days.

- A) 7 days
- B) 15 days
- C) 30 days ✅
- D) 60 days

> **Explanation:** In Gmail, emails moved to the Trash folder are automatically and permanently deleted after 30 days. Similarly, spam emails are also auto-deleted after 30 days.

---

### Q14. Which of the following is NOT a valid email address?

- A) rahul@gmail.com
- B) priya_sharma@yahoo.com
- C) amit@techpath.biz
- D) sneha gmail.com ✅

> **Explanation:** Every valid email address must contain exactly one @ symbol separating the username from the domain. 'sneha gmail.com' is missing the @ symbol, making it an invalid email address.

---

### Q15. Netiquette refers to ___.

- A) Network security protocols
- B) Internet etiquette or rules of polite online behavior ✅
- C) Email encryption method
- D) A type of email service

> **Explanation:** Netiquette is short for Network Etiquette (or Internet Etiquette). It refers to the informal rules and guidelines for polite and respectful behavior when communicating on the internet.
