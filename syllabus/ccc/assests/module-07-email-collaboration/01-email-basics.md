## What is Email?

**Email** (Electronic Mail) is a method of sending and receiving messages over the internet using electronic devices like computers, smartphones, and tablets. It is one of the oldest and most widely used internet services.

Think of email as a digital version of a physical letter. Instead of writing on paper and posting it at a post office, you type your message on a computer and send it instantly over the internet.

**CCC Exam Tip:** Email stands for **Electronic Mail**. This is a frequently asked question. Remember that email allows you to send text messages, documents, images, and other files electronically.

---

## Email vs Physical Mail

Understanding the difference between email and traditional postal mail helps you appreciate the power of digital communication.

| Feature | Physical Mail (Post) | Email (Electronic Mail) |
|---------|---------------------|------------------------|
| Speed | Days to weeks | Seconds to minutes |
| Cost | Postage stamps, courier charges | Free (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook) |
| Format | Paper, envelope | Digital text, attachments |
| Delivery | Postman delivers to address | Delivered to email inbox |
| Storage | Physical files and drawers | Digital folders on server |
| Attachments | Physical documents in envelope | Digital files (PDF, images, docs) |
| Address format | House No, Street, City, PIN | username@domain.com |
| Receipt confirmation | No automatic confirmation | Read receipts available |
| Copies | Photocopying needed | CC and BCC (instant copies) |
| Environmental impact | Uses paper and fuel | Eco-friendly (no paper) |

**CCC Exam Tip:** Questions comparing email with postal mail frequently appear. Remember the key advantages: **speed, cost (free), reliability, and ability to send attachments**.

## Email Address Format

Every email user has a unique email address. The format of an email address is:

```
username@domain.com
```

### Parts of an Email Address

An email address has three important parts:

1. **Username** (Local Part) — The name you choose when creating your account
   - Example: `rahul.sharma`, `priya2024`, `amit_tech`
   - Can contain letters, numbers, dots (.), underscores (_), and hyphens (-)
   - Cannot contain spaces or special characters like # or !

2. **@ Symbol** (At Sign) — The separator between username and domain
   - Every email address must have exactly one @ symbol
   - It separates "who" (username) from "where" (domain)

3. **Domain Name** — The email service provider's address
   - Examples: `gmail.com`, `yahoo.com`, `outlook.com`, `rediffmail.com`
   - The domain includes a TLD (Top Level Domain) like `.com`, `.in`, `.org`

### Examples of Email Addresses

| Email Address | Username | Domain |
|--------------|----------|--------|
| rahul.sharma@gmail.com | rahul.sharma | gmail.com |
| priya_student@yahoo.com | priya_student | yahoo.com |
| amit@techpath.biz | amit | techpath.biz |
| sneha2024@outlook.com | sneha2024 | outlook.com |

**CCC Exam Tip:** "The @ symbol in an email address separates ___" is a very common question. Answer: **It separates the username from the domain name**. Also remember that every email address must have exactly ONE @ symbol.

## Creating a Gmail Account

Follow these steps to create a free Gmail account:

### Step 1: Open the Gmail Website
- Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox)
- Type `www.gmail.com` in the address bar
- Click **Create account**

### Step 2: Enter Your Details
- **First name:** Enter your first name (e.g., Rahul)
- **Last name:** Enter your last name (e.g., Sharma)
- **Username:** Choose a unique username (e.g., rahul.sharma2024)
  - Gmail will tell you if the username is already taken
  - If taken, try adding numbers or dots

### Step 3: Set a Password
- Create a strong password (at least 8 characters)
- Use a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols
- Example of a strong password: `Rahul@2024Bpl`
- Confirm your password by typing it again

### Step 4: Add Recovery Information
- **Phone number:** Add your mobile number for account recovery
- **Recovery email:** Add another email address (optional)
- **Date of birth:** Enter your birth date
- **Gender:** Select your gender

### Step 5: Accept Terms and Conditions
- Read Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
- Click **I Agree** to create your account

### Step 6: Your Account is Ready!
- Your new email address is: `rahul.sharma2024@gmail.com`
- You can now send and receive emails

**CCC Exam Tip:** To create a Gmail account, you need a **username, password, phone number, and date of birth**. The minimum age for creating a Gmail account in India is **13 years**.

## Features of Email

Email offers many useful features that make digital communication convenient:

1. **Free to Use** — Services like Gmail, Yahoo Mail, and Outlook are completely free
2. **Instant Delivery** — Messages are delivered within seconds
3. **Attachments** — You can send files, images, documents, and videos along with your message
4. **Multiple Recipients** — Send one email to many people at once using CC and BCC
5. **24/7 Availability** — You can send and receive emails anytime, anywhere
6. **Storage** — Emails are stored on servers; you can access them from any device
7. **Searchable** — Find old emails easily using the search bar
8. **Eco-Friendly** — No paper is wasted
9. **Record Keeping** — Every email is automatically saved as a record of communication
10. **Global Reach** — Send emails to anyone in the world with an email address

## Advantages and Disadvantages of Email

### Advantages
- Fast and reliable communication
- Free or very low cost
- Can send attachments (documents, photos, videos)
- Accessible from anywhere with internet
- Automatic record of all conversations
- Can reach multiple people simultaneously

### Disadvantages
- Requires internet connection
- Risk of spam (unwanted emails) and phishing attacks
- Large attachments may not be delivered (size limits apply)
- Can be impersonal compared to face-to-face communication
- Risk of virus transmission through infected attachments
- Some people may not check email regularly
