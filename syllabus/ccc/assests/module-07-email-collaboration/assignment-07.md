## Task 1: Create a Gmail Account and Send Your First Email

**Objective:** Learn to create an email account and compose a professional email.

### Steps:
1. Open Google Chrome and go to **www.gmail.com**
2. Click **Create Account** and follow the registration process:
   - Enter your first and last name
   - Choose a username (try: `yourname.ccc2026@gmail.com`)
   - Set a strong password (mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols)
   - Add your mobile number for recovery
   - Enter your date of birth and gender
   - Accept terms and conditions
3. After creating the account, click **Compose**
4. Write a professional email to your trainer with the following details:
   - **To:** Your trainer's email address
   - **Subject:** "CCC Assignment 07 - Email Basics - [Your Name]"
   - **Body:** Write a formal email introducing yourself. Include:
     - A proper greeting (Respected Sir/Ma'am)
     - Your name, batch, and roll number
     - Why you enrolled in the CCC course
     - A polite closing (Thank you, Regards)
5. Click **Send**

**What you practiced:** Email account creation, composing, subject line, professional writing.

### Screenshot needed: Your Sent folder showing the email you sent.

## Task 2: Practice CC, BCC, and Attachments

**Objective:** Understand the difference between To, CC, and BCC, and learn to send attachments.

### Steps:
1. Open Gmail and click **Compose**
2. Prepare the following email:
   - **To:** Your own email address (the one you just created)
   - **CC:** A classmate's email address (ask a fellow student for their email)
   - **BCC:** Your trainer's email address
   - **Subject:** "CC and BCC Practice - [Your Name]"
   - **Body:** Write a short message explaining:
     - Who can see the To recipient? (Answer: Everyone)
     - Who can see the CC recipient? (Answer: Everyone)
     - Who can see the BCC recipient? (Answer: No one except the sender)
3. **Attach a file:**
   - Open Notepad and type: "This is my first email attachment for CCC Module 07"
   - Save it as `my_first_attachment.txt` on your Desktop
   - In the email compose window, click the **paperclip icon**
   - Select and attach the file
4. Click **Send**
5. Open the received email in your Inbox and check:
   - Can you see the CC recipient's address? (Yes)
   - Can you see the BCC recipient's address? (No)

**What you practiced:** To, CC, BCC fields, sending attachments, understanding recipient visibility.

### Screenshots needed:
- The compose window showing To, CC, BCC fields
- The received email showing CC is visible but BCC is not

## Task 3: Organize Your Inbox with Labels and Stars

**Objective:** Learn mailbox management techniques that improve productivity.

### Steps:

#### Part A: Create Labels
1. In Gmail, click the **gear icon** (top right) → **See all settings**
2. Go to the **Labels** tab
3. Create the following three labels:
   - `CCC Studies`
   - `Personal`
   - `Important Documents`
4. Go back to your Inbox

#### Part B: Apply Labels and Stars
1. Find the email you sent in Task 2
2. Right-click on it → **Label as** → Select `CCC Studies`
3. Click the **star icon** next to the email to mark it as important
4. Click on **Starred** in the left sidebar — verify your email appears there
5. Click on the `CCC Studies` label in the sidebar — verify the email appears there

#### Part C: Search for Emails
Practice using Gmail search operators:
1. In the search bar, type: `from:me` — this shows all emails you sent
2. Type: `has:attachment` — this shows emails with attachments
3. Type: `subject:CCC` — this shows emails with "CCC" in the subject
4. Type: `is:starred` — this shows all starred emails

**What you practiced:** Creating labels, starring emails, using search operators.

### Screenshots needed:
- Your Labels list showing the three new labels
- The search results for `has:attachment`
