# Browser Configuration

**Module 06 — CCC Exam Preparation | Topic 2**

---

## Introduction

A web browser is not just for opening websites — you can customize and configure it to suit your needs. In this topic, you will learn how to set your homepage, manage privacy settings, handle cookies, use incognito mode, and manage extensions. These are important topics for the CCC exam.

---

## Setting the Homepage

The **homepage** is the first web page that opens when you start your browser or click the Home button.

### How to Set Homepage in Google Chrome:
1. Click the **three dots** (menu) in the top-right corner
2. Go to **Settings**
3. In the left sidebar, click **On startup**
4. Choose one of these options:
   - **Open the New Tab page** — Shows a blank tab with your most visited sites
   - **Continue where you left off** — Opens the tabs you had when you last closed Chrome
   - **Open a specific page or set of pages** — Type the URL you want as homepage
5. To add a homepage, click **Add a new page** and type the URL (e.g., `https://www.google.com`)

**Common homepage choices in India:**
- Google.com (most popular)
- Your organization's website
- A news website (Times of India, NDTV)
- Your email service (Gmail)

**CCC Exam Tip:** The homepage is the first page that opens when you start your browser. You can set any website as your homepage through browser settings.

## Setting Default Browser

The **default browser** is the one that opens automatically when you click any link outside the browser (like a link in an email or document).

### How to Set Default Browser in Windows 10/11:
1. Press **Windows + I** to open Settings
2. Go to **Apps** > **Default Apps**
3. Under "Web browser," click the current browser
4. Select your preferred browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, etc.)

**CCC Exam Tip:** The default browser is different from the homepage. Default browser = which browser opens. Homepage = which website opens first in that browser.

## Privacy Settings

Web browsers have privacy settings that control how much information websites can collect about you.

### Important Privacy Settings in Chrome:

**1. Safe Browsing**
- Protects you from dangerous websites and downloads
- Location: Settings > Privacy and Security > Security
- Options: Enhanced protection, Standard protection, No protection
- **Recommendation:** Keep at least "Standard protection" enabled

**2. Do Not Track**
- Sends a request to websites asking them not to track your browsing activity
- Location: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
- Note: Websites may choose to ignore this request

**3. Site Settings**
- Control what websites can access on your computer:
  - **Camera:** Allow or block websites from using your webcam
  - **Microphone:** Allow or block audio recording
  - **Location:** Allow or block websites from knowing your location
  - **Notifications:** Allow or block popup notifications
- Location: Settings > Privacy and Security > Site Settings

**CCC Exam Tip:** Privacy settings help protect your personal information. Always review what permissions you give to websites.

## Cookies

### What are Cookies?

**Cookies** are small text files that websites store on your computer. They remember information about your visit so the website can provide a better experience next time.

**Real-life analogy:** Think of cookies like a restaurant that remembers your favorite dish. When you visit again, they already know what you like.

### What Cookies Do:

| Type | Purpose | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Session Cookies** | Remember you during one visit; deleted when you close browser | Keeping items in your IRCTC shopping cart |
| **Persistent Cookies** | Stay on your computer for days/months; remember preferences | Keeping you logged into Gmail |
| **Third-party Cookies** | Placed by advertisers on other websites to track you across sites | Seeing ads for shoes on Facebook after searching for shoes on Amazon |

### Managing Cookies:

**How to Accept/Block Cookies in Chrome:**
1. Go to **Settings** > **Privacy and Security** > **Cookies and other site data**
2. Choose one of these options:
   - **Allow all cookies** — All websites can save cookies (least private)
   - **Block third-party cookies** — Blocks advertiser tracking (recommended)
   - **Block all cookies** — No cookies saved (most private but many sites will not work properly)

**How to Delete Cookies:**
1. Go to **Settings** > **Privacy and Security** > **Clear browsing data**
2. Check the box for **Cookies and other site data**
3. Select time range (last hour, last 24 hours, all time)
4. Click **Clear data**

**CCC Exam Tip:** "What are cookies in browser?" is a very common CCC question. Answer: Cookies are small text files stored on your computer by websites to remember your preferences and login information.

## Browser History

**Browser history** is a list of all websites you have visited. The browser records every page you visit with the date and time.

### Viewing History:
- Press **Ctrl + H** to open history
- Or click Menu (three dots) > **History** > **History**

### Clearing History:
1. Press **Ctrl + Shift + Delete** (opens Clear Browsing Data dialog)
2. Select what to clear:
   - **Browsing history** — List of websites visited
   - **Cookies and other site data** — Login info and preferences
   - **Cached images and files** — Saved copies of web pages for faster loading
3. Select the time range (last hour, last day, last week, all time)
4. Click **Clear data**

**Why clear history?**
- Privacy — others using the same computer cannot see what you visited
- Free up storage space
- Fix website loading problems
- Remove saved passwords you no longer need

**CCC Exam Tip:** Ctrl + H opens browser history. Ctrl + Shift + Delete opens the clear browsing data dialog.

## Incognito / Private Browsing Mode

**Incognito mode** (called **Private Browsing** in Firefox, **InPrivate** in Edge) is a special browsing mode that does not save your browsing data.

### What Incognito Mode DOES:
- Does NOT save browsing history
- Does NOT save cookies (deleted when you close the incognito window)
- Does NOT save form data or passwords
- Does NOT appear in your browser history

### What Incognito Mode does NOT DO:
- Does NOT make you invisible on the internet
- Does NOT hide your activity from your ISP (Internet Service Provider)
- Does NOT hide your activity from your employer or school network
- Does NOT protect you from malware or phishing

### How to Open Incognito Mode:
| Browser | Shortcut | Name |
|---------|----------|------|
| Chrome | Ctrl + Shift + N | Incognito |
| Firefox | Ctrl + Shift + P | Private Browsing |
| Edge | Ctrl + Shift + N | InPrivate |
| Opera | Ctrl + Shift + N | Private Window |

**When to use incognito mode:**
- Searching for something personal on a shared/public computer
- Logging into accounts on someone else's device
- Checking flight/hotel prices without cookies affecting the price
- Testing how a website looks without cached data

**CCC Exam Tip:** Incognito mode does not save history and cookies locally, but your ISP and network admin can still see your activity. It does NOT make you anonymous on the internet.

## Managing Extensions / Add-ons

**Extensions** (also called **add-ons** in Firefox) are small software programs that add extra features to your browser.

### Popular Browser Extensions:

| Extension | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **AdBlock Plus** | Blocks advertisements on websites |
| **Grammarly** | Checks spelling and grammar while typing |
| **LastPass** | Manages and generates strong passwords |
| **Google Translate** | Translates web pages to your language |
| **Dark Reader** | Makes websites dark-themed to reduce eye strain |

### How to Install Extensions in Chrome:
1. Click Menu (three dots) > **Extensions** > **Visit Chrome Web Store**
2. Search for the extension you want
3. Click **Add to Chrome**
4. Confirm by clicking **Add Extension**

### How to Remove Extensions:
1. Click Menu > **Extensions** > **Manage Extensions**
2. Find the extension you want to remove
3. Click **Remove** and confirm

**Safety tip:** Only install extensions from the official Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons page. Untrusted extensions can steal your data.

**CCC Exam Tip:** Extensions add extra features to your browser. They can be installed from the browser's official store (Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons).
