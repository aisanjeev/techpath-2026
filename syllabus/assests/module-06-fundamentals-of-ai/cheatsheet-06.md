## Prompt Engineering Tips

1. **Be specific** — say exactly what you want
2. **Set format** — "as a table / 5 bullet points"
3. **Give context** — "for beginners / for a resume"
4. **Set length** — "in 100 words"
5. **Give examples** — show desired output
6. **Iterate** — refine if first answer isn't right

## AI Safety Rules

- Always **verify** AI-generated facts
- Don't trust AI for medical/legal/financial advice
- AI can **hallucinate** (make things up)
- AI learns **biases** from biased data
- Use AI as a **tool**, not replacement for learning
