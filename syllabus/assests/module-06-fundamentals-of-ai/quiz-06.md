# Quiz: Fundamentals of AI

**Module 06 | 12 Questions | Pass Mark: 60%**

---

## Q1. What is Artificial Intelligence?

- A) A robot that looks like a human
- B) Making computers do tasks that normally need human intelligence ✅
- C) A type of programming language
- D) A super-fast computer

> **Explanation:** AI means making computers perform tasks that would normally require human intelligence — like understanding language, recognizing images, or making decisions.

---

## Q2. Which type of AI exists today?

- A) General AI
- B) Super AI
- C) Narrow AI ✅
- D) Conscious AI

> **Explanation:** All current AI is Narrow AI — good at one specific task. General AI (good at everything like humans) doesn't exist yet.

---

## Q3. What is Machine Learning?

- A) A machine that learns from textbooks
- B) AI that learns patterns from data instead of being given rules ✅
- C) A programming language
- D) A type of computer hardware

> **Explanation:** In Machine Learning, instead of programming rules, you give the computer data and examples so it can find the patterns (rules) itself.

---

## Q4. In supervised learning, training data must have:

- A) Only images
- B) Labels (correct answers) ✅
- C) No labels at all
- D) Only numbers

> **Explanation:** Supervised learning needs labeled data — examples with the correct answer. Like flash cards with questions AND answers.

---

## Q5. What does LLM stand for?

- A) Long Learning Machine
- B) Large Language Model ✅
- C) Linked Logic Module
- D) Low Latency Memory

> **Explanation:** LLM = Large Language Model. ChatGPT and Claude are LLMs — trained on huge amounts of text to understand and generate language.

---

## Q6. What is an AI hallucination?

- A) When AI sees things that aren't there
- B) When AI confidently gives wrong or made-up information ✅
- C) When AI shuts down
- D) When AI runs too slowly

> **Explanation:** AI hallucination is when it generates confident-sounding but incorrect or completely made-up information. Always verify important facts.

---

## Q7. What is Generative AI?

- A) AI that generates electricity
- B) AI that creates new content like text, images, and code ✅
- C) AI that copies existing content
- D) AI that deletes data

> **Explanation:** Generative AI creates NEW content — text (ChatGPT), images (DALL-E), code (Copilot), music (Suno).

---

## Q8. What makes a good AI prompt?

- A) One word is enough
- B) Being specific about what you want, format, and audience ✅
- C) Using technical jargon
- D) Making it as long as possible

> **Explanation:** Good prompts are specific — include what you want, the format, the audience, and any constraints.

---

## Q9. What is a neural network inspired by?

- A) The internet
- B) The human brain ✅
- C) A computer chip
- D) A phone network

> **Explanation:** Neural networks are inspired by the human brain — layers of connected "neurons" that process information and find patterns.

---

## Q10. Which is a major ethical concern about AI?

- A) AI uses too much electricity
- B) AI can learn biases from biased training data ✅
- C) AI is too expensive
- D) AI is too slow

> **Explanation:** AI can learn unfair biases from biased training data. For example, a hiring AI trained mostly on men's resumes might unfairly reject women.

---

## Q11. What is a token in AI context?

- A) A payment coin
- B) A small piece of text that AI reads and processes ✅
- C) A security password
- D) A type of computer memory

> **Explanation:** AI reads text as tokens — small word pieces. Models have a context window (max tokens they can process at once).

---

## Q12. What should you always do with AI-generated information?

- A) Trust it completely
- B) Share it immediately
- C) Verify it from a reliable source ✅
- D) Delete it

> **Explanation:** Always verify AI-generated information from reliable sources. AI can hallucinate and give wrong facts confidently.
