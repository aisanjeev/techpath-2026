## Task 3: Prompt Engineering Challenge (30 marks)

Use ChatGPT or Claude. For each task, write a prompt and paste both the prompt AND the result:

1. **Simple**: Get AI to explain "recursion" to a 10-year-old
2. **Formatted**: Get AI to create a comparison table of Python vs JavaScript (at least 8 rows)
3. **Role-based**: Get AI to act as an Indian HR manager and conduct a mock interview (3 questions + ideal answers)
4. **Creative**: Get AI to write a marketing email for TechPath Institute's new ADCA batch
5. **Technical**: Get AI to write a Python function that validates an Indian phone number (10 digits, starts with 6-9)
6. **Refined**: Take your worst result from 1-5, improve the prompt, and show the better output. Explain what you changed.

## Task 4: AI Ethics Case Study (25 marks)

Choose ONE real-world AI ethics incident (from the list below or your own research) and write a 300-word analysis:

**Options:**
- Amazon's AI hiring tool that discriminated against women (2018)
- Google Gemini's image generation controversy (2024)
- Deepfake videos of public figures
- AI-generated art vs human artists (copyright debate)
- ChatGPT in education — cheating or learning tool?

**Your analysis should include:**
1. What happened (facts)
2. What went wrong (technical/ethical failure)
3. Who was affected and how
4. What could have been done differently
5. Your personal opinion with reasoning
