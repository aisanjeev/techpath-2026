How Binary Conversion Works

1 Each binary digit has a place value (like ones, tens in decimal, but powers of 2):
8 | 4 | 2 | 1
2 Example: Convert 1101 to decimal
1×8 + 1×4 + 0×2 + 1×1 = 13
3 Example: Convert decimal 10 to binary
10 ÷ 2 = 5 remainder 0
5 ÷ 2 = 2 remainder 1
2 ÷ 2 = 1 remainder 0
1 ÷ 2 = 0 remainder 1
Read bottom to top: 1010
