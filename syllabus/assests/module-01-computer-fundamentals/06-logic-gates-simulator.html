Quick Memory Trick

AND = Both must be 1 → "A AND B must BOTH be true"

OR = At least one is 1 → "A OR B, either one is enough"

NOT = Flip the value → "NOT 0 = 1, NOT 1 = 0"

NAND = Opposite of AND → "NOT AND"

NOR = Opposite of OR → "NOT OR"

XOR = Different inputs = 1 → "Exactly one must be true"
