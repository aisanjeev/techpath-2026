## Task 1: Excel — Sales Dashboard (35 marks)

Create an Excel workbook with the following:

**Sheet 1 — Raw Data:** Enter this sales data (or download from provided link):

| Date | Salesperson | Region | Product | Units | Price per Unit |
|------|------------|--------|---------|-------|---------------|
| (At least 50 rows of sample data across 3 months, 4 salespeople, 3 regions, 5 products) |

**Sheet 2 — Formulas:** Calculate:
1. Total revenue for each salesperson (SUMIF)
2. Average units sold per region (AVERAGEIF)
3. Highest single-day sale (MAX)
4. Count of sales above Rs 10,000 (COUNTIF)
5. Commission at 5% of each salesperson's total (formula)
6. Salesperson rank by total revenue (RANK)

**Sheet 3 — Pivot Table:** Create a pivot table showing:
- Rows: Salesperson
- Columns: Month
- Values: Total Revenue
- Add a slicer for Region

**Sheet 4 — Dashboard:** Create 3 charts:
1. Bar chart — Revenue by salesperson
2. Line chart — Monthly sales trend
3. Pie chart — Revenue by region
