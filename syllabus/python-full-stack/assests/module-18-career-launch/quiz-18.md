## Q1. What is the recommended number of pinned repositories on a GitHub profile for a fresher developer?

- A) 2
- B) 5 ✓
- C) 10
- D) 15

> **Explanation:** The 5-repo strategy recommends pinning 5 repositories: one full-stack app, one AI/ML project, one open source contribution, one scripting/automation tool, and one personal/creative project.

---

## Q2. What does ATS stand for in the context of job applications?

- A) Automated Testing System
- B) Application Tracking Software
- C) Applicant Tracking System ✓
- D) Advanced Talent Search

> **Explanation:** ATS stands for Applicant Tracking System. It is software used by companies to filter resumes before a human recruiter sees them.

---

## Q3. Why do resumes with tables, images, and fancy layouts often get rejected by companies?

- A) Recruiters prefer plain resumes
- B) ATS software cannot parse complex formatting ✓
- C) Colorful resumes are unprofessional
- D) Companies have a strict formatting policy

> **Explanation:** ATS software reads resumes as plain text. Tables, images, icons, and complex layouts cannot be parsed correctly, causing the resume to appear blank or garbled to the system.

---

## Q4. What is the ideal length of a fresher developer's resume?

- A) Half a page
- B) 1 page ✓
- C) 2 pages
- D) 3 pages

> **Explanation:** For a fresher with 0-2 years of experience, the resume should be exactly 1 page. All relevant information should fit within a single page using concise language.

## Q5. Which data structure would you use to solve the Two Sum problem efficiently?

- A) List (Array)
- B) Stack
- C) Dictionary (HashMap) ✓
- D) Queue

> **Explanation:** A dictionary (HashMap) allows O(1) lookups. For each number, you check if the complement (target minus current number) exists in the dictionary, giving an overall O(n) time complexity.

---

## Q6. What is the time complexity of Binary Search?

- A) O(n)
- B) O(n^2)
- C) O(log n) ✓
- D) O(1)

> **Explanation:** Binary Search divides the sorted array in half with each comparison, resulting in O(log n) time complexity. This is much faster than linear search O(n) for large datasets.

## Q7. In the STAR method for answering behavioral interview questions, what does the 'A' stand for?

- A) Analysis
- B) Action ✓
- C) Assessment
- D) Approach

> **Explanation:** STAR stands for Situation, Task, Action, Result. The 'A' (Action) is where you describe what you specifically did to address the task or challenge.

## Q8. What is the recommended best time to post on LinkedIn for maximum visibility in India?

- A) 12:00 AM - 1:00 AM IST
- B) 8:00 AM - 9:00 AM IST ✓
- C) 2:00 PM - 3:00 PM IST
- D) 11:00 PM - 12:00 AM IST

> **Explanation:** The best times to post on LinkedIn in India are 8:00 AM to 9:00 AM IST (morning) or 6:00 PM to 7:00 PM IST (evening), when professionals are most active on the platform.

## Q9. On Upwork, what is the commission rate for the first $500 earned from a single client?

- A) 5%
- B) 10%
- C) 15%
- D) 20% ✓

> **Explanation:** Upwork charges 20% commission for the first $500 earned from a client, then 10% for earnings between $500 and $10,000, and 5% for earnings above $10,000 with the same client.

## Q10. Which of the following is an ethical use of AI during job hunting?

- A) Using AI to answer live coding tests during an interview
- B) Copy-pasting AI-generated cover letters without reading them
- C) Using AI to practice mock interview questions and refine answers ✓
- D) Claiming AI-built projects as your own original work

> **Explanation:** Using AI to practice mock interviews is ethical because you are learning and improving your own skills. The other options involve dishonesty or misrepresentation.

## Q11. What is the LinkedIn Social Selling Index (SSI) target score recommended for active job seekers?

- A) 20 or above
- B) 35 or above
- C) 50 or above ✓
- D) 80 or above

> **Explanation:** A target SSI score of 50 or above is recommended for job seekers. The SSI measures profile completeness, search activity, content engagement, and relationship building on a 0-100 scale.

## Q12. When a freelancer's annual income in India exceeds what amount, GST registration becomes mandatory?

- A) 5 lakh
- B) 10 lakh
- C) 20 lakh ✓
- D) 50 lakh

> **Explanation:** GST registration is mandatory in India when a freelancer's annual income exceeds 20 lakh (10 lakh for some states). Below this threshold, registration is optional.

## Q13. What is the correct approach when you cannot solve a coding problem during a technical interview?

- A) Stay silent and keep thinking
- B) Tell the interviewer you do not know and wait
- C) Explain your thought process and attempt a brute-force solution ✓
- D) Ask the interviewer to give you the answer

> **Explanation:** Thinking out loud and attempting a brute-force solution shows problem-solving ability even if you cannot find the optimal solution. Interviewers evaluate your approach and communication, not just the final answer.

## Q14. Which type of resume bullet point is preferred by both ATS systems and recruiters?

- A) Worked on a web application using Python
- B) Responsible for backend development tasks
- C) Built a REST API with 25 endpoints serving 100+ users using FastAPI ✓
- D) Participated in team activities and contributed to projects

> **Explanation:** Option C uses a strong action verb (Built), includes specific numbers (25 endpoints, 100+ users), and mentions the technology (FastAPI). This format is both ATS-friendly and impressive to human reviewers.

## Q15. What should you do within 24 hours after completing a job interview?

- A) Call the interviewer to ask about the result
- B) Send a thank-you email to the interviewer ✓
- C) Post about the interview on social media
- D) Apply to 10 more companies immediately

> **Explanation:** Sending a thank-you email within 24 hours shows professionalism and keeps you fresh in the interviewer's memory. It is a simple gesture that many candidates skip, giving you an advantage.
