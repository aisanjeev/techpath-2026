## Why WhatsApp Marketing is Huge in India

India has 550+ million WhatsApp users — more than any other country in the world. Your customers are already on WhatsApp every day, chatting with friends, family, and now businesses. Unlike email that might sit unopened for hours, a WhatsApp message gets read within 5 minutes on average.

Think of WhatsApp marketing like having a direct conversation at the customer's doorstep versus email marketing which is like dropping a letter in their mailbox. Both work, but WhatsApp feels personal and immediate.

| Metric | Email | WhatsApp | SMS |
|--------|-------|----------|-----|
| Open Rate | 20-25% | 95-98% | 90-95% |
| Click Rate | 2-5% | 35-45% | 5-10% |
| Response Time | Hours/Days | Minutes | Minutes |
| Cost per Message | ₹0.10-0.50 | ₹0.50-1.50 | ₹0.15-0.25 |
| Rich Media | Limited | Images, videos, PDFs, buttons | Text only (160 chars) |
| Best For | Nurturing, newsletters | Engagement, support, commerce | Alerts, OTPs, flash sales |

> **Real Example:** Meesho, the Indian social commerce platform, uses WhatsApp to onboard new resellers. They send product catalogs, order updates, and business tips — all via WhatsApp. It helped them grow to 150 million+ users because their target audience (small town resellers) uses WhatsApp far more than email.

## WhatsApp Business App vs WhatsApp Business API

These are two very different products. Understanding the difference is critical.

| Feature | WhatsApp Business App | WhatsApp Business API |
|---------|----------------------|----------------------|
| **Cost** | Free | Paid (per conversation) |
| **Best For** | Small businesses, solo sellers | Medium to large businesses |
| **Max Users** | 1 phone, up to 4 linked devices | Unlimited team members |
| **Broadcast Limit** | 256 contacts per list | Unlimited (with opt-in) |
| **Automation** | Basic auto-replies, quick replies | Full chatbots, workflows |
| **Catalog** | Yes (up to 500 products) | Yes (via API integration) |
| **Green Tick** | Not available | Available (after verification) |
| **Integration** | None — standalone app | CRM, e-commerce, helpdesk |
| **Setup** | Download app, verify number | Apply through BSP (Business Solution Provider) |

**When to upgrade from App to API:**
- You are sending more than 256 broadcasts regularly
- You need multiple team members replying to customers
- You want automated chatbot flows
- You need integration with your CRM or e-commerce platform

## WhatsApp Template Messages

The WhatsApp Business API requires you to use pre-approved templates for starting conversations. You cannot just send any message to any customer — Meta reviews and approves each template first.

**Three categories of templates:**

| Category | Purpose | Example | Cost (India) |
|----------|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Marketing** | Promotions, offers, product launches | "Hi Priya! Our Summer Sale is LIVE. Get 40% off on all courses." | ₹0.80-1.00/message |
| **Utility** | Order updates, payment reminders | "Your order #4521 has been shipped. Track here: [link]" | ₹0.35-0.50/message |
| **Authentication** | OTPs, login verification | "Your verification code is 482916. Valid for 10 minutes." | ₹0.25-0.35/message |

**Template approval tips:**
- Keep language simple and clear
- Always include an opt-out option ("Reply STOP to unsubscribe")
- Do not use ALL CAPS or excessive exclamation marks
- Avoid words like "free," "winner," "guaranteed" — they get rejected
- Approval takes 1-24 hours (usually under 2 hours)

## Broadcast vs Group Messaging

| Feature | Broadcast | Group |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Privacy | Contacts cannot see each other | All members see each other |
| Replies | Come to you privately | Go to the entire group |
| Limit | 256 per list (App) / Unlimited (API) | 1,024 members max |
| Control | You control who receives | Members can add others |
| Best For | Promotions, announcements | Community building, discussions |

**Golden rule:** Use broadcasts for marketing messages and groups for community building. Never add customers to groups without permission — it feels invasive and they will leave immediately.

## WhatsApp Catalog and Commerce

WhatsApp now lets businesses create a product catalog inside the app. Customers can browse, ask questions, and even place orders without leaving WhatsApp.

**Setting up a catalog:**
1. Open WhatsApp Business → Settings → Business Tools → Catalog
2. Add products with images, descriptions, and prices
3. Share catalog link or individual products in chats

**WhatsApp Commerce features (2026):**
- In-app product browsing
- Cart functionality (customers add multiple items)
- Payment integration (UPI payments via WhatsApp Pay in India)
- Order management from the business dashboard

> **Real Example:** JioMart processes over 5 lakh orders per day through WhatsApp Commerce. Customers in tier-2 and tier-3 cities who find apps confusing can simply text "I want dal and rice" and get product suggestions with one-tap ordering.

## Conversational Marketing Flows

Conversational marketing means using real-time conversations (WhatsApp, chatbots, live chat) to guide customers through the buying journey instead of making them fill forms and wait for a callback.

**Example flow for coaching institute in Pune:**

```
Customer clicks WhatsApp ad
  → Bot: "Hi! Welcome to ABC Coaching. What are you preparing for?"
    → [UPSC] [CAT] [Bank Exams] [Other]
      → Customer taps: CAT
        → Bot: "Great! Our next CAT batch starts Aug 15.
                Fee: ₹45,000 (EMI available).
                Want to book a free demo class?"
          → [Yes, book demo] [Send me details] [Talk to counselor]
            → Routes to appropriate next step
```

This flow runs 24/7 and captures leads even at 2 AM when your team is asleep.

## SMS Marketing in India — DLT Registration

SMS marketing in India is regulated by TRAI (Telecom Regulatory Authority of India). Since 2021, all businesses must register on the DLT (Distributed Ledger Technology) platform before sending any commercial SMS.

**DLT registration process:**
1. Choose a DLT operator: Jio DLT, Airtel DLT, Vodafone Idea DLT, or BSNL DLT
2. Register your business entity (PAN, GST, or Aadhaar for sole proprietors)
3. Register your sender header (e.g., "TPACAD" for TechPath Academy — 6 characters)
4. Submit SMS templates for approval
5. Get content templates scrubbed and approved

**SMS types in India:**

| Type | Header Format | Example | When |
|------|--------------|---------|------|
| **Promotional** | Starts with number (e.g., 1-TPACAD) | "Summer sale! 50% off all courses" | 9 AM - 9 PM only |
| **Transactional** | Starts with letter (e.g., TM-TPACAD) | "Your OTP is 493827" | Any time, 24/7 |
| **Service Implicit** | Starts with letter (e.g., SI-TPACAD) | "Your policy renewal is due" | Any time, existing customers |
| **Service Explicit** | Starts with letter (e.g., SE-TPACAD) | "New feature launched, check it out" | Any time, with consent |

**Bulk SMS pricing in India:**

| Volume | Price per SMS | Monthly Cost (10K SMS) |
|--------|-------------|----------------------|
| 1,000-10,000 | ₹0.20-0.25 | ₹2,000-2,500 |
| 10,000-50,000 | ₹0.15-0.20 | ₹1,500-2,000 |
| 50,000-1,00,000 | ₹0.12-0.15 | ₹1,200-1,500 |
| 1,00,000+ | ₹0.08-0.12 | ₹800-1,200 |

## When to Use WhatsApp vs SMS vs Email

| Scenario | Best Channel | Why |
|----------|-------------|-----|
| Weekly newsletter with 5 articles | **Email** | Long content, links, formatting |
| Flash sale ending in 4 hours | **WhatsApp** or **SMS** | Immediate open, urgency |
| Order shipped notification | **WhatsApp** | Rich media (tracking link, image) |
| OTP / login verification | **SMS** | Universal, works without internet |
| Abandoned cart recovery | **Email** + **WhatsApp** | Email for detail, WhatsApp for urgency |
| Customer support | **WhatsApp** | Real-time, rich media, conversation |
| Monthly billing reminder | **SMS** + **Email** | SMS for alert, email for invoice |
| Product catalog / lookbook | **WhatsApp** | Native catalog, visual browsing |
| Drip nurture sequence (7+ emails) | **Email** | Automation, segmentation, A/B testing |
| Event RSVP / appointment reminder | **WhatsApp** | Interactive buttons, high open rate |

> **Pro Tip:** The most effective strategy uses all three channels together. Send an email with full details, a WhatsApp reminder with a quick link, and an SMS as a last-resort nudge before a deadline.

## Push Notifications Basics

Push notifications are messages sent directly to a user's phone or browser, even when they are not using your app or website.

| Type | Where It Appears | Example |
|------|-----------------|---------|
| **Mobile Push** | Phone lock screen / notification panel | Swiggy: "Your favourite biryani is 30% off today!" |
| **Web Push** | Browser notification | Blog: "New article: 10 Instagram Growth Hacks" |
| **In-App** | Inside the app while using it | Flipkart: "Your item's price just dropped!" |

**Push notification best practices:**
- Keep it under 50 characters (gets truncated on most phones)
- Send 2-3 per week maximum (more causes uninstalls)
- Personalize with the user's name or behavior
- Time it right — 10 AM and 7 PM work best in India
- Always provide value, not just promotions
