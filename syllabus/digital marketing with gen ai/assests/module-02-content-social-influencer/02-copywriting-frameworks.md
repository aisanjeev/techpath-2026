## What is Copywriting?

Copywriting is writing words that make people **take action** — click a button, buy a product, sign up for a newsletter, or download an app. It is different from content writing (which educates or entertains). Copywriting has one job: **conversion**.

Think of copywriting like a salesperson in written form. A great salesperson at a Reliance Digital store does not just list specifications — they understand your need, show how the product solves your problem, and make you feel confident about buying. Great copy does the same thing.

> **Fun Fact:** The subject line of an email is just 5-10 words — but it determines whether 1,000 people or 10,000 people read your message. That is the power of copywriting.

## Framework 1: AIDA — Attention, Interest, Desire, Action

The most classic copywriting framework. Used in ads, landing pages, emails, and sales pages for over 100 years.

| Step | What to Do | Goal |
|------|-----------|------|
| **A** — Attention | Grab them with a bold headline or hook | Stop the scroll |
| **I** — Interest | Share a fact, statistic, or story that connects to their problem | Keep them reading |
| **D** — Desire | Show them what life looks like AFTER using your product | Make them WANT it |
| **A** — Action | Tell them exactly what to do next (CTA) | Get the click/purchase |

### AIDA Example 1: Online Coding Course

```
[ATTENTION]  "90% of IT freshers struggle to crack their first interview."

[INTEREST]   "Most colleges teach theory, not practical coding. Employers want 
              you to build real projects — not recite textbook definitions."

[DESIRE]     "Our students build 5 real-world projects in 12 weeks and get 
              placement support at 500+ companies. Average package: ₹4.5 LPA."

[ACTION]     "Start your free trial today — no credit card needed."
```

### AIDA Example 2: Fitness App

```
[ATTENTION]  "Tired of gym memberships you never use?"

[INTEREST]   "Research shows 73% of gym members in India stop going within 
              3 months. The problem is not your motivation — it is not having 
              a plan that fits YOUR schedule."

[DESIRE]     "FitBuddy gives you a personalized 20-minute workout plan you 
              can do at home. 2 lakh+ users. 4.7-star rating. No equipment needed."

[ACTION]     "Download free on Play Store — your first month is on us."
```

### AIDA Example 3: D2C Protein Brand

```
[ATTENTION]  "Why does your protein powder taste like chalk?"

[INTEREST]   "Most Indian protein brands add cheap fillers and artificial 
              sweeteners. You are paying ₹2,000+ for something your body 
              barely absorbs."

[DESIRE]     "MuscleBlend uses whey isolate with 0 fillers, comes in real 
              mango and cold coffee flavours, and dissolves in 10 seconds. 
              50,000+ gym-goers switched last year."

[ACTION]     "Try the starter pack at ₹499 — satisfaction guaranteed or 
              full refund."
```

## Framework 2: PAS — Problem, Agitate, Solution

PAS works by identifying a pain point, making the reader feel it deeply, and then presenting your product as the relief. Very effective for ads and email marketing.

| Step | What to Do | Goal |
|------|-----------|------|
| **P** — Problem | State the problem your audience faces | "I feel seen" |
| **A** — Agitate | Twist the knife — show what happens if they do NOT solve it | "I need to fix this NOW" |
| **S** — Solution | Present your product/service as the answer | "This is exactly what I need" |

### PAS Example 1: Resume Writing Service

```
[PROBLEM]    "Applied to 50 jobs and heard nothing back?"

[AGITATE]    "Every day you wait, 500 other freshers with better resumes are 
              applying to the same roles. Your resume gets 6 seconds of 
              attention from a recruiter. If the format, keywords, and 
              summary are not perfect — it goes straight to the reject pile. 
              Meanwhile, your batch-mates are getting interview calls."

[SOLUTION]   "ResumeKraft builds ATS-optimized resumes that actually get 
              shortlisted. 15,000+ professionals helped. Average time to 
              first interview call: 12 days. Get yours for ₹999."
```

### PAS Example 2: Accounting Software for Small Business

```
[PROBLEM]    "Still managing your business accounts in Excel?"

[AGITATE]    "One formula error and your entire GST filing goes wrong. 
              Notices from the tax department. Late fees piling up. Hours 
              wasted every month on data entry instead of growing your 
              business. And when CA season comes, you are scrambling to 
              find that one missing invoice from September."

[SOLUTION]   "BillDesk automates invoicing, GST filing, and expense tracking 
              for Indian small businesses. Takes 10 minutes to set up. 
              ₹500/month — less than what one GST penalty costs. Start 
              free for 30 days."
```

### PAS Example 3: English Speaking Course

```
[PROBLEM]    "Do you freeze up when you have to speak English in a meeting?"

[AGITATE]    "Your ideas are brilliant — but when you try to explain them 
              in English, the words do not come out right. You see colleagues 
              with less knowledge getting promoted because they speak 
              confidently. Every presentation makes your heart race. 
              Every interview feels like a test you are not prepared for."

[SOLUTION]   "SpeakEasy is a 30-day spoken English program designed for 
              Indian professionals. Live practice sessions with trained 
              coaches. 10,000+ professionals now speak confidently at work. 
              Join the next batch — starts Monday."
```

## Framework 3: BAB — Before, After, Bridge

BAB paints a picture of the customer's life BEFORE and AFTER your solution, then bridges the gap with your product.

| Step | What to Do |
|------|-----------|
| **Before** | Describe the current painful/frustrating situation |
| **After** | Describe the ideal future once the problem is solved |
| **Bridge** | Show how your product/service gets them from Before to After |

### BAB Example: Project Management Tool

```
[BEFORE]     "Your team uses WhatsApp groups, email chains, and shared 
              Google Docs to manage projects. Deadlines get missed. Files 
              get lost. Nobody knows who is responsible for what."

[AFTER]      "Imagine opening one dashboard and seeing every project, every 
              deadline, every team member's progress — all in one place. 
              No more 'did you see my message?' No more missed deadlines."

[BRIDGE]     "TaskFlow brings your entire team onto one platform. Kanban 
              boards, deadline tracking, file sharing, and automated 
              reminders. 500+ Indian startups already use it. 
              Free for teams under 10."
```

## Framework 4: The 4U Formula

Used primarily for **headlines and subject lines**. Every headline should score high on at least 2-3 of these.

| U | Stands For | What It Means | Example |
|---|-----------|---------------|---------|
| **U** | Urgent | Creates a time pressure | "Offer ends tonight" |
| **U** | Unique | Stands out from similar content | "The only marketing trick that costs ₹0" |
| **U** | Useful | Promises a clear benefit | "How to double your Instagram reach" |
| **U** | Ultra-Specific | Uses numbers, details, specifics | "7 tools that saved me 3 hours every day" |

### Headline Before/After Using 4U

| Before (Weak) | After (4U Applied) | Which U's? |
|--------------|-------------------|------------|
| "Marketing tips" | "7 Marketing Tricks That Doubled Our Sales in 30 Days" | Useful, Ultra-Specific |
| "Learn digital marketing" | "Free Webinar Today: Learn the 3 Ads Strategies Spending Under ₹5,000/month" | Urgent, Useful, Ultra-Specific |
| "New product launch" | "Only 100 Units Available: Our Best-Selling Serum Now in Travel Size" | Urgent, Unique, Ultra-Specific |

## Headline Writing Techniques

Headlines determine whether people read or scroll past. Here are proven techniques:

### The Numbers Technique

Numbers make headlines specific and scannable.

| Without Numbers | With Numbers |
|----------------|-------------|
| "Ways to save money" | "**7** ways to save **₹10,000** every month" |
| "Digital marketing tools" | "**12** free tools every digital marketer needs in **2026**" |
| "Improve your Instagram" | "How I grew from **0** to **5,000** followers in **30** days" |

### The Power Words Technique

Power words trigger emotion and curiosity.

| Category | Power Words |
|----------|------------|
| **Curiosity** | Secret, hidden, surprising, unexpected, revealed |
| **Urgency** | Now, today, limited, last chance, deadline, hurry |
| **Trust** | Proven, guaranteed, research-backed, certified, tested |
| **Value** | Free, save, bonus, exclusive, instant, effortless |
| **Fear** | Mistake, avoid, warning, never, stop, before it is too late |

### The Curiosity Gap Technique

Tell them ENOUGH to be curious, but not enough to be satisfied without clicking.

| Too vague (no click) | Too complete (no click needed) | Just right (curiosity gap) |
|---------------------|-------------------------------|--------------------------|
| "Marketing update" | "Google updated its algorithm to prefer mobile-fast sites" | "Google just changed its algorithm — here is what it means for your traffic" |
| "New study" | "80% of Indian millennials prefer Instagram Reels" | "80% of Indian millennials prefer THIS content format (it is not what you think)" |

## CTA (Call to Action) Writing

A CTA tells the reader exactly what to do next. Weak CTAs lose conversions.

### CTA Formula: Action Verb + Benefit + Urgency

| Weak CTA | Strong CTA | Why It Is Better |
|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| "Submit" | "Get My Free Guide" | Benefit-focused |
| "Click here" | "Start My Free Trial" | Action + benefit |
| "Learn more" | "See How It Works (2-min Video)" | Reduces friction |
| "Buy now" | "Grab It Before Midnight — Only 23 Left" | Urgency + scarcity |
| "Sign up" | "Join 10,000+ Marketers — It's Free" | Social proof + benefit |

## Ad Copy vs Landing Page Copy vs Email Copy

Each format has different rules because the reader's context is different.

| Element | Ad Copy | Landing Page Copy | Email Copy |
|---------|---------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Length** | 1-3 lines (very short) | Long-form (scroll-worthy) | Medium (150-300 words) |
| **Goal** | Stop the scroll, get the click | Convince and convert | Nurture relationship, get the click |
| **Tone** | Punchy, bold, attention-grabbing | Persuasive, detailed, benefit-heavy | Personal, conversational, like a friend |
| **CTA** | One clear button (Shop Now, Learn More) | One primary CTA repeated 2-3 times | One clear link or button |
| **Visuals** | Image/video does 80% of work | Hero image + testimonials + screenshots | Minimal — text-focused |
| **Framework** | AIDA or PAS (compressed) | Full AIDA or PAS | PAS or BAB |

## Trainer Activity: Copy Rewrite Challenge

**Time:** 15 minutes

Show these weak copies on screen. Students rewrite them using any framework (AIDA, PAS, BAB, or 4U).

1. "Best digital marketing course available online" — Rewrite for a fresher audience
2. "Download our fitness app" — Rewrite for busy working professionals
3. "Buy handmade jewellery" — Rewrite for young women on Instagram

Each student reads their best rewrite aloud. Class votes on the most compelling version.

## Trainer Activity: Headline Battle

**Time:** 10 minutes

Give the class ONE product: "A ₹999 Bluetooth speaker with 12-hour battery life, IPX5 waterproof, available in 5 colours."

Each student writes 3 headlines using 3 different techniques:
1. One using **numbers**
2. One using **power words**
3. One using the **curiosity gap**

Collect and read the best ones. Discuss what makes each one work.

## Summary

- **AIDA** (Attention-Interest-Desire-Action) — the classic framework for ads and landing pages
- **PAS** (Problem-Agitate-Solution) — most effective when your audience has a strong pain point
- **BAB** (Before-After-Bridge) — great for showing transformation stories
- **4U Formula** (Urgent-Unique-Useful-Ultra-Specific) — perfect for headlines and subject lines
- Use **numbers, power words, and curiosity gaps** in your headlines
- CTAs should include an **action verb + benefit + urgency**
- **Ad copy** is short and punchy, **landing page copy** is detailed and persuasive, **email copy** is personal and conversational
- Always rewrite your first draft — the **second version is always better**
- Study Indian brand copy daily — observe what makes you click, save, or buy
