## ASCI (Advertising Standards Council of India) Guidelines

| Reference | URL | What It Covers |
|-----------|-----|---------------|
| ASCI Code | https://ascionline.in/index.php/code-and-guidelines | Full advertising code |
| Influencer Guidelines | https://ascionline.in/index.php/influencer-advertising-guidelines | Disclosure requirements |
| File a Complaint | https://ascionline.in/index.php/lodge-complaint | How to report violations |

**Key Rules:**
- All paid partnerships require visible disclosure (#Ad, #Sponsored, #PaidPartnership)
- Disclosure must be in the first line or clearly visible (not hidden in hashtags)
- Both the brand and influencer are responsible for compliance
