## Task 1: Content Repurposing — 1 Blog Post into 10 Pieces (25 Marks)

### Objective
Take one pillar blog post and repurpose it into 10 different content pieces across multiple platforms — proving you understand platform-native content adaptation.

### What to Do

1. **Write (or use AI to draft) one pillar blog post:**

   - Must follow E-E-A-T guidelines
   - Must include at least 3 H2 headings, examples, and a conclusion
   - Indian context required (Indian examples, ₹ pricing, local references)

2. **Repurpose into 10 content pieces:**

   | # | Format | Platform | Requirement |
   |---|--------|----------|-------------|
   | 1 | Carousel (10 slides) | Instagram/LinkedIn | Full slide text + design notes |
   | 2 | Short-form video script | Instagram Reels/YouTube Shorts | 30-60 second script with visual cues |
   | 3 | Twitter/X thread | Twitter | 7-10 tweets, each under 280 chars |
   | 4 | LinkedIn text post | LinkedIn | 200-300 words with hook and CTA |

   | 7 | Poll/question | LinkedIn or Instagram Stories | 1 poll with 4 options |
   | 8 | Infographic outline | Pinterest/LinkedIn | Title + 5-7 data points |
   | 9 | YouTube video outline | YouTube | Title, thumbnail idea, 5 talking points |
   | 10 | WhatsApp broadcast message | WhatsApp Business | Under 100 words, with CTA |

3. **Each piece must:**
   - Stand alone (make sense without reading the blog)
   - Match the platform's tone and format
   - Have a clear CTA

### Deliverables

## Task 2: Instagram Carousel Design — "5 Digital Marketing Myths" (25 Marks)

### Objective
Design a complete 10-slide Instagram carousel in Canva that busts 5 common digital marketing myths. This tests your visual content creation and copywriting skills.

### What to Do

1. **Plan your carousel structure:**
   - Slide 1: Hook slide (curiosity-driven title)
   - Slide 2: Context/intro (who this is for)
   - Slides 3-7: One myth per slide (myth + truth)
   - Slide 8: Summary/recap
   - Slide 9: Key takeaway
   - Slide 10: CTA (follow, save, share) + your handle

2. **Choose 5 myths to bust (examples):**
   - "You need lakhs to start digital marketing"
   - "More followers = more money"
   - "SEO is dead because of AI"
   - "You need to post every single day"
   - "Digital marketing does not work for local businesses"

3. **Design requirements:**
   - Size: 1080 x 1080 px (square) or 1080 x 1350 px (portrait)
   - Use consistent brand colors and fonts throughout
   - Text must be readable without zooming
   - One main idea per slide (not cluttered)
   - Include your name/handle on the last slide

4. **Caption:**
   - Write a full Instagram caption to accompany the carousel
   - Use Hook-Body-CTA structure
   - Include 10-15 relevant hashtags

### Deliverables
- Canva link (shareable) to the carousel
- Exported PNG/PDF of all 10 slides
- Caption (ready to post)
- A short note: What design decisions did you make and why?

### Marking Criteria
| Criteria | Marks |
|----------|-------|
| Content quality (myths are real, truths are accurate) | 6 |
| Visual design (consistency, readability, aesthetics) | 7 |
| Copywriting on slides (concise, punchy, clear) | 5 |
| Hook slide effectiveness (would you stop scrolling?) | 4 |
| Caption quality (Hook-Body-CTA + hashtags) | 3 |

## Task 3: Influencer Outreach DMs — Local Clothing Brand (25 Marks)

### Objective
Write 10 personalized influencer outreach DMs for a fictional local clothing brand. Each DM should target a different type of influencer and demonstrate personalization skills.

### What to Do

1. **The brand:**
   - Name: "Desi Threads" — a sustainable clothing brand in Bhopal
   - Niche: Affordable ethnic and fusion wear for women aged 18-35
   - Price range: ₹499 to ₹2,999
   - USP: Handloom fabrics, eco-friendly packaging, made by local artisans
   - Budget for influencer marketing: ₹50,000/month
   - Goal: Increase Instagram followers and drive online sales

2. **Write 10 outreach DMs for these influencer types:**

   | # | Influencer Type | Follower Range | Compensation Model |
   |---|----------------|---------------|-------------------|
   | 1 | Nano fashion influencer (local) | 2K-5K | Barter (free products) |
   | 2 | Micro fashion influencer | 15K-30K | Barter + ₹3,000 |
   | 3 | College student with style content | 5K-10K | Free products + affiliate code |
   | 4 | Sustainable living influencer | 10K-50K | ₹5,000 + products |
   | 5 | Local city page/community | 20K-80K | ₹8,000 for one post |
   | 6 | Mom blogger who posts fashion | 10K-25K | Barter + ₹2,000 |
   | 7 | Festive/wedding content creator | 30K-60K | ₹10,000 for a Reel |
   | 8 | Male fashion influencer (for gifting angle) | 5K-15K | Barter only |
   | 9 | YouTube vlogger (city lifestyle) | 20K-50K | ₹7,000 + products |
   | 10 | LinkedIn thought leader (brand story angle) | 5K-15K | ₹5,000 for a post |

3. **Each DM must include:**
   - Personalized opening (reference their specific content)
   - Clear explanation of why you are reaching out to THEM specifically
   - What you are proposing (deliverables, compensation)
   - CTA (next step)
   - Professional sign-off

4. **Rules:**
   - No two DMs should use the same opening line
   - Each must reference something specific about that influencer type
   - Keep each DM under 150 words
   - Do NOT be generic or template-sounding

### Deliverables
- All 10 DMs in one document (clearly labeled by influencer type)
- A brief influencer strategy note (200 words): How would you choose which influencers to actually reach out to? What metrics would you check?
- For any 2 DMs, write a follow-up message (sent 3 days later if no response)

### Marking Criteria

## Task 4: 30-Day Social Media Campaign Plan — Real Local Business (25 Marks)

### Objective
Create a comprehensive 30-day social media campaign plan for a real local business in your city. This is your capstone project for Module 2.

### What to Do

1. **Choose a real local business:**
   - Must be a business you can visit or research online
   - Examples: local restaurant, coaching centre, gym, salon, clothing store, bakery
   - Get permission if possible (or use their public information)

2. **Audit their current social media:**
   - Which platforms are they on?
   - How often do they post?
   - What is their engagement rate? (likes + comments / followers x 100)
   - What type of content do they currently create?
   - What is their brand voice?
   - Identify 3 strengths and 3 weaknesses

3. **Create the campaign plan:**

   **Campaign Brief (1 page):**
   - Campaign name and theme
   - Objective (specific, measurable goal)
   - Target audience (1 detailed buyer persona)
   - Platforms (max 2-3, with reasoning)
   - Budget (realistic for a local business: ₹5,000-₹20,000)
   - Duration: 30 days
   - Key messages (3 things you want the audience to know)

   **Content Calendar (30 days):**
   - Day, post type, content topic, format, platform, CTA
   - Follow the 4-1-1 rule
   - Include at least: 5 Reels/Shorts, 3 carousels, 2 Stories series

   **Sample Content (create 5 pieces):**
   - 2 carousel slide decks (designed in Canva)
   - 2 Reel/Shorts scripts (with visual directions)
   - 1 full caption with hashtags

   **Measurement Plan:**
   - KPIs you will track (follower growth, engagement rate, reach, clicks)
   - Tools you will use to track
   - How often you will check metrics
   - What "success" looks like after 30 days

4. **Bonus (extra 5 marks):**
   - Actually execute at least 7 days of the campaign (with screenshots as proof)
   - Show before/after metrics for those 7 days

### Deliverables
- Business audit document (current state analysis)
- Campaign brief (1 page)
- 30-day content calendar (spreadsheet or table)
- 5 content pieces (2 carousels + 2 scripts + 1 caption)
- Measurement plan
- (Bonus) Execution proof with screenshots

### Marking Criteria
| Criteria | Marks |
|----------|-------|
| Business audit quality (thorough, honest assessment) | 5 |
| Campaign brief (clear objective, realistic plan) | 5 |
| Content calendar (complete, follows 4-1-1, variety) | 5 |
| Sample content quality (platform-native, creative) | 6 |
| Measurement plan (right KPIs, realistic targets) | 4 |
| (Bonus) Execution with proof | +5 |
