AIDA Formula Builder

Attention → Interest → Desire → Action. Write copy for an Indian coaching class.

Start with a bold question, shocking stat, or pain point. Keep under 15 words.
Share a surprising fact, case study, or unique angle that makes them want to read more.
Paint the outcome. Use sensory language and social proof. What will their life look like?
Clear CTA with urgency or scarcity. One specific action only.

PAS Formula Builder

Problem → Agitate → Solution. Write copy for an online saree shop.

State the problem your customer faces. Be specific — generic problems get ignored.
Twist the knife. Make the problem feel worse. What happens if they do nothing?
Present your product as the relief. Include proof and a clear next step.
