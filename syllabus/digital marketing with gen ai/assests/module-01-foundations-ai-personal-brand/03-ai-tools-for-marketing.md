## Why AI is a Marketer's Superpower

AI tools do not replace marketers — they make marketers 10x faster. Tasks that used to take hours (writing blog posts, creating ad variations, designing social media graphics) now take minutes. The marketer who knows how to use AI effectively will always beat the one who does everything manually.

## The CRAFT Framework for Writing AI Prompts

Most people write vague prompts like "Write me a social media post." This gives generic, useless results. The CRAFT framework helps you write prompts that give you exactly what you need.

| Letter | Stands For | What It Means | Example |
|--------|-----------|--------------|---------|
| **C** | Context | Background information the AI needs | "I run a D2C skincare brand for Indian women aged 20-35" |
| **R** | Role | What persona should the AI adopt | "Act as an experienced social media copywriter" |
| **A** | Action | What specifically you want the AI to do | "Write 5 Instagram carousel hooks" |
| **F** | Format | How you want the output structured | "Each hook should be under 10 words, use emojis, include a CTA" |
| **T** | Tone | The voice and style of the output | "Casual, friendly, like talking to a best friend — not salesy" |

## 5 Marketing Prompts Using CRAFT

### Prompt 1: Social Media Captions

```
Context: I run "FreshBasket," an organic grocery delivery startup in Hyderabad. 
We deliver farm-fresh vegetables within 2 hours.

Role: Act as a social media manager for a health-focused food brand.

Action: Write 7 Instagram captions (one for each day of the week) promoting our 
farm-fresh vegetables.

Format: Each caption should have:
- A hook (first line that stops the scroll)
- 2-3 lines of body text
- 3-5 relevant hashtags
- A CTA (call to action)

Tone: Warm, friendly, health-conscious. Use simple Hindi-English mix where natural 
(like "ghar ka khana" or "fresh sabzi").
```

### Prompt 2: Email Subject Lines

```
Context: TechPath Academy is launching a new "Digital Marketing with AI" course 
priced at ₹15,000. Early bird discount of 30% ends in 3 days.

Role: Act as an email marketing specialist with high open rate expertise.

Action: Write 15 email subject lines to promote this course to working 
professionals aged 22-30 in India.

Format: Each subject line should be under 50 characters. Group them into 
3 categories: curiosity-based (5), urgency-based (5), benefit-based (5).

Tone: Professional but not boring. Create FOMO without being spammy.
```

### Prompt 3: Blog Post Outline

```
Context: I write for a personal finance blog targeting Indian millennials. 
The topic is "How to start investing with just ₹500."

Role: Act as a content strategist who understands SEO and reader engagement.

Action: Create a detailed blog post outline with 8-10 sections.

Format: For each section, include:
- Section heading (H2)
- 3-4 bullet points of what to cover
- One Indian example or statistic to include
- Suggested internal/external links

Tone: Conversational, encouraging, like an older sibling giving advice. 
Avoid jargon — explain every financial term.
```

### Prompt 4: Ad Copy Variations

```
Context: boAt is launching a new wireless earbuds model at ₹1,499. Key features: 
48-hour battery, IPX5 water resistance, quad mic for calls, bass boost.

Role: Act as a performance marketing copywriter who writes high-converting 
Facebook ads.

Action: Write 5 different Facebook ad copy variations targeting college 
students aged 18-24 in India.

Format: Each variation should have:
- Headline (under 40 characters)
- Primary text (under 125 characters)
- Description (under 30 characters)
- CTA button text

Tone: Energetic, youthful, using Gen-Z language. Reference gym, commute, 
and gaming use cases.
```

### Prompt 5: Competitor Analysis

```
Context: I am building a new meal-kit delivery service in Bangalore, 
competing with EatFit, Rebel Foods, and FreshMenu.

Role: Act as a market research analyst specializing in Indian food-tech.

Action: Analyze the positioning of these 3 competitors and suggest 5 
differentiation opportunities for my brand.

Format: Create a comparison table with columns: Brand, Target Audience, 
Price Range, USP, Weakness. Then list 5 opportunities with explanations.

Tone: Analytical and data-driven. Support claims with reasoning.
```

## Trainer Activity: Prompt Writing Challenge

**Time:** 15 minutes

Split the class into groups of 3-4. Each group picks a scenario:

1. **Scenario A:** You are launching a coworking space in Pune for freelancers
2. **Scenario B:** You run a wedding photography studio in Jaipur
3. **Scenario C:** You sell homemade pickles and chutneys online from Lucknow

Each group writes ONE prompt using the CRAFT framework for their scenario. They then paste it into ChatGPT or Claude and share the output with the class.

Discuss: Which group got the best output? What made their prompt better?

## When to Use AI vs Human Creativity

AI is powerful, but it is not a replacement for everything. Here is when to use each:

| Task | Use AI | Use Human Creativity | Why |
|------|--------|---------------------|-----|
| First draft of a blog post | Yes | No (initially) | AI writes 80% in minutes; you polish the rest |
| Brand strategy and positioning | No | Yes | Requires deep understanding of culture, emotion, market |
| Ad copy variations | Yes | Review needed | AI generates 20 options; human picks the best 3 |
| Social media captions | Yes | Light editing | AI handles volume; human adds authentic voice |
| Crisis communication | No | Yes | Requires empathy, legal awareness, brand sensitivity |
| Data analysis and reports | Yes | Interpretation | AI crunches numbers; human explains what they mean |
| Competitor research | Yes | Strategy from it | AI gathers data; human creates competitive strategy |
| Visual design concepts | Both | Final direction | AI generates options; human refines the chosen direction |
| Customer reply to a complaint | No | Yes | Requires genuine empathy and brand-appropriate response |
| SEO keyword research | Yes | Strategy from it | AI finds keywords; human plans the content strategy |

> **Pro Tip:** The best workflow is "AI creates, human curates." Use AI for the first 80% (speed), then use your brain for the final 20% (quality, authenticity, brand voice).

## Common Mistakes When Using AI for Marketing

| Mistake | What Happens | How to Avoid It |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| Copy-pasting AI output without editing | Content sounds generic and robotic | Always edit, add personal voice, fact-check |
| Not giving enough context in prompts | AI gives vague, unusable output | Use the CRAFT framework every time |
| Using AI for everything | Loses authentic brand voice | Keep human creativity for strategy and emotional content |
| Not fact-checking AI claims | AI can generate wrong statistics | Verify every number, date, and claim |
| Same prompt for every platform | Instagram caption ≠ LinkedIn post | Specify the platform and audience in your prompt |
| Ignoring AI ethics | Plagiarism, misinformation, copyright issues | Always disclose AI use when required, verify originality |
