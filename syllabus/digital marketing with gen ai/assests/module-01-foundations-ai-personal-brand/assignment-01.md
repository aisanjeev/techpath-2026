## Task 2: AI-Powered Buyer Personas for a Local Business (25 Marks)

### Objective
Use ChatGPT to create 3 detailed buyer personas for a real local business in Mughalsarai (or your nearest town).

### What to Do

1. **Choose a local business** (examples: a kirana store going online, a coaching centre, a sweet shop, a gym, a salon, a clothing store)

2. **Research the business:**
   - Visit the shop or check their Google listing
   - Note what they sell, who visits, price range
   - Observe their current marketing (or lack of it)

3. **Use ChatGPT to create 3 buyer personas** using the CRAFT framework:
   - Persona 1: Their primary customer (most common buyer)
   - Persona 2: Their ideal customer (highest value buyer)
   - Persona 3: An untapped customer segment (new opportunity)

4. **Each persona must include:**
   - Indian name, age, city/area, income level
   - Occupation and family status
   - Goals and frustrations
   - Daily media habits (which apps, when)
   - What would trigger them to buy
   - Objections they might have
   - Best way to reach them (platform + content type)

5. **Document your prompts:**
   - Save all ChatGPT prompts you used
   - Show at least 2 rounds of iteration (refining the output)

### Deliverables
- Name and brief description of the chosen business
- 3 complete buyer persona documents (1 page each)
- Screenshot of your ChatGPT conversation (showing CRAFT prompts)
- 1 paragraph: How would you use these personas in a marketing campaign?

### Marking Criteria
| Criteria | Marks |
|----------|-------|
| Business selection and research | 5 |
| Persona 1 — depth and realism | 5 |
| Persona 2 — depth and realism | 5 |
| Persona 3 — creativity (untapped segment) | 5 |
| Prompt quality (CRAFT framework used, iteration shown) | 5 |

## Task 3: 30-Day LinkedIn Content Calendar (25 Marks)

### Objective
Create a complete 30-day LinkedIn content calendar using the 4-1-1 rule (for every 6 posts: 4 value, 1 promotional, 1 personal/story).

### What to Do

1. **Define your content pillars** (choose 3-5 topics you will post about):
   - Example: AI Tools, Digital Marketing Tips, Career Growth, Personal Journey, Industry News

2. **Plan 30 days of content** (posting 5 days a week = 22 posts, or daily = 30 posts — choose your frequency):
   
   For each post, specify:
   - Day and date
   - Content pillar
   - Post type: Value (educational) / Promotional / Personal
   - Post format: Text, Carousel, Poll, Video, Image
   - Topic/Title (one line)
   - Hook (first line of the post)
   - CTA (what you want readers to do)

3. **Follow the 4-1-1 Rule:**
   - 4 out of every 6 posts = educational/value content
   - 1 out of every 6 posts = promotional (your skills, services, portfolio)
   - 1 out of every 6 posts = personal story/journey

4. **Write 3 full posts** (not just the plan — write the complete post for any 3 days)

5. **Use AI to help:**
   - Use ChatGPT/Claude to brainstorm topics
   - Use AI to generate hook variations
   - Document which AI prompts you used

### Deliverables
- Spreadsheet or table with complete 30-day calendar
- 3 fully written LinkedIn posts (ready to publish)
- List of content pillars with reasoning
- Screenshots showing AI tool usage for brainstorming

### Marking Criteria
| Criteria | Marks |
|----------|-------|
| Content pillars clearly defined (with reasoning) | 5 |
| 30-day calendar complete with all required fields | 7 |
| 4-1-1 rule correctly applied | 4 |
| 3 full posts written (quality, hooks, CTAs) | 6 |
| AI tools used effectively (prompts shown) | 3 |
