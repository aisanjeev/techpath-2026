Marketing Math Calculator

Calculate key metrics and see how they compare to Indian industry benchmarks

CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)

CAC = Total Marketing Spend / New Customers

LTV (Customer Lifetime Value)

LTV = Avg Purchase x Frequency x Lifespan

ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)

ROAS = Revenue from Ads / Ad Spend

CTR (Click-Through Rate)

CTR = (Clicks / Impressions) x 100

CPC / CPM Calculator

CPC = Spend/Clicks | CPM = (Spend/Impressions) x 1000

Break-Even Calculator

Break-Even = Fixed Costs / (Price - Variable Cost)
