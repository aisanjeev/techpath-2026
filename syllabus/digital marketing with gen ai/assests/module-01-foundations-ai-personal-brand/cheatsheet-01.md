## Marketing Math Formulas

| Metric | Formula | Good Benchmark |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| CAC | Total Marketing Spend / New Customers | Lower is better; LTV should be 3x CAC |
| LTV | Avg Order Value x Frequency x Lifespan | Higher is better |
| ROAS | Revenue from Ads / Ad Spend | 3x+ is healthy |
| ROI | (Revenue - Cost) / Cost x 100 | 100%+ means profitable |
| CPC | Total Ad Spend / Total Clicks | ₹5-50 (depends on industry) |
| CTR | (Clicks / Impressions) x 100 | 2-5% is good |
| CPM | (Ad Spend / Impressions) x 1000 | ₹50-300 (India avg) |
| Conversion Rate | (Conversions / Visitors) x 100 | 2-5% for websites |

**Golden Rule:** LTV > 3x CAC = Profitable business

## CRAFT Framework (Prompt Engineering)

```
C — Context     → Background info about your business/situation
R — Role        → Who should the AI pretend to be?
A — Action      → What exactly should it do?
F — Format      → How should the output look?
T — Tone        → What style/voice to use?
```

**Quick Example:**
```
C: I run a gym in Pune for working professionals
R: You are a social media copywriter  
A: Write 5 Instagram Reels ideas for January fitness motivation
F: Table with columns: Idea | Hook | Duration | CTA
T: Energetic, motivational, use Hinglish
```

## Marketing Funnel (TOFU/MOFU/BOFU)

```
TOFU (Awareness)  → Blogs, Reels, Social Posts → Metric: Reach, Views
MOFU (Consideration) → Emails, Webinars, Guides → Metric: Leads, Engagement  
BOFU (Decision)   → Demos, Offers, Testimonials → Metric: Conversions, Sales
```

## SMART Goals Checklist

- **S**pecific: What exactly will you achieve?
- **M**easurable: What number will you track?
- **A**chievable: Can you realistically do this?
- **R**elevant: Does this matter for your bigger goal?
- **T**ime-bound: By when?

Example: "Reach 500 LinkedIn followers by posting 5x/week for 60 days"

## The 4-1-1 Rule (Content Mix)

For every 6 posts:
- 4 VALUE posts (tips, tutorials, insights)
- 1 PROMO post (your services, skills, portfolio)
- 1 PERSONAL post (your story, lessons, failures)
