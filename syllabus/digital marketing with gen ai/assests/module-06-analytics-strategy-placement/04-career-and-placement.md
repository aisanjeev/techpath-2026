## Digital Marketing Job Roles Explained

Digital marketing is not one job — it is a family of specialized roles. Understanding each role helps you choose where to focus and how to position yourself.

| Role | What You Do Daily | Skills Needed |
|------|------------------|--------------|
| **SEO Specialist** | Keyword research, on-page optimization, technical audits, backlink building | GA4, Search Console, SEMrush/Ahrefs, HTML basics |
| **Content Marketer** | Blog writing, content strategy, editorial calendar, content distribution | Writing, SEO basics, CMS (WordPress), research |
| **Social Media Manager** | Creating posts, community management, scheduling, trend analysis | Canva, scheduling tools, copywriting, analytics |
| **Paid Ads Specialist** | Google Ads, Meta Ads, campaign setup, bid management, A/B testing | Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, Excel, analytics |
| **Email Marketing Manager** | Campaign creation, automation, segmentation, deliverability management | Mailchimp/Brevo, copywriting, A/B testing, HTML email |
| **Marketing Analyst** | Data analysis, dashboards, reporting, conversion optimization | GA4, Looker Studio, Excel/Sheets, SQL basics |
| **Growth Hacker** | Rapid experimentation, funnel optimization, virality, retention | Multi-channel, data analysis, automation tools, creativity |
| **Digital Marketing Manager** | Managing all channels, team coordination, strategy, budgets | Leadership, strategy, cross-channel knowledge |

Think of these roles like positions in a cricket team. The opener (SEO Specialist) focuses on building a strong foundation. The fast bowler (Paid Ads Specialist) brings quick results. The captain (DM Manager) coordinates everything. As a fresher, being a good all-rounder makes you most valuable.

## Resume for ATS (Applicant Tracking System)

90% of medium and large companies in India use ATS software (like Freshteam, Zoho Recruit, or Greenhouse) to filter resumes before a human ever sees them. If your resume is not ATS-friendly, it gets rejected automatically.

### ATS Resume Format Rules

| Rule | Do This | Avoid This |
|------|---------|------------|
| **Layout** | Single column, clean structure | Two columns, creative layouts, infographics |
| **Font** | Arial, Calibri, or Times New Roman | Decorative fonts, cursive, custom fonts |
| **File Format** | PDF (or .docx if requested) | Images, JPEG resume, Canva-designed PDFs |
| **Headers** | Standard: Experience, Education, Skills, Certifications | Creative headers like "My Journey" or "What I Bring" |
| **Contact Info** | Name, phone, email, LinkedIn, city | Photo, date of birth, gender, marital status |
| **Keywords** | Match keywords from the job description | Generic skills not mentioned in the JD |

### Quantified Achievements

The biggest mistake freshers make is listing responsibilities instead of achievements. Use numbers wherever possible.

| Weak (Responsibility) | Strong (Achievement) |
|----------------------|---------------------|
| "Managed social media accounts" | "Grew Instagram from 2K to 15K followers in 6 months" |
| "Handled email marketing" | "Increased email open rate from 15% to 28% through A/B testing" |
| "Worked on SEO" | "Improved organic traffic by 150% (5K to 12.5K monthly visits) in 4 months" |
| "Created content" | "Published 40 blog posts generating 50,000+ organic page views" |
| "Ran Google Ads" | "Managed ₹3L monthly ad spend, achieving 4.2x ROAS" |
| "Assisted with campaigns" | "Contributed to Diwali campaign that generated ₹8L in sales" |

> **Fun Fact:** Resumes with quantified achievements get 40% more interview calls. Recruiters spend an average of 7 seconds on a resume — numbers jump out instantly, paragraphs do not.

## LinkedIn Optimization for Job Hunting

LinkedIn is where 87% of recruiters search for candidates. Your LinkedIn profile is your digital resume that works 24/7.

**Profile optimization checklist:**

| Section | What to Write | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Headline** | Role you want + key skill + value | "Digital Marketing Executive | SEO & Paid Ads | Helping brands grow organic traffic" |
| **About** | 3-4 paragraphs: who you are, what you do, what you are looking for | Story format with keywords from target job descriptions |
| **Experience** | Same as resume — quantified achievements | "Grew client's Instagram from 0 to 10K in 90 days" |
| **Featured** | Pin your best work — case studies, certificates, projects | Portfolio link, campaign results screenshot, certificate image |
| **Skills** | Add 50 skills, pin top 3 that match your target role | Digital Marketing, SEO, Google Ads, Content Marketing |
| **Recommendations** | Get 3-5 from managers, professors, or clients | Request from internship supervisor or project collaborator |

**LinkedIn activity for job hunting:**
- Post 2-3 times per week about what you are learning (builds visibility)
- Comment thoughtfully on posts by marketing leaders and recruiters
- Follow and engage with companies you want to work at
- Use "Open to Work" frame (visible to recruiters only option available)
- Send personalized connection requests to hiring managers (not "I'd like to connect")

## Portfolio Building

Your portfolio proves you can do the work. Certifications show knowledge; a portfolio shows application.

**What to include in your portfolio:**

| Portfolio Piece | What It Demonstrates | How to Create It |
|----------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| **SEO Case Study** | Keyword research, on-page optimization, traffic growth | Optimize a friend's website or a personal blog; show before/after |
| **Social Media Campaign** | Content creation, scheduling, engagement growth | Run a 30-day growth experiment on your own Instagram/LinkedIn |
| **Google Ads Report** | Campaign setup, keyword selection, ROAS | Use Google Ads ₹20,000 free credit offer for new accounts |
| **Email Sequence** | Copywriting, automation setup, open rates | Build a 5-email sequence in Mailchimp free tier for a fictional brand |
| **Content Samples** | Writing ability, SEO knowledge, audience understanding | 3-5 published blog posts (on Medium or personal blog) |
| **Analytics Dashboard** | Data analysis, visualization, insights | Create a Looker Studio dashboard with sample GA4 data |

**Where to host your portfolio:**
- Personal website (build with WordPress, Wix, or AI tools like Bolt)
- Notion page (free, easy to update, shareable link)
- Behance (better for visual/design work)
- Google Drive folder (last resort — not professional-looking)

## 30 Common Interview Questions with Answer Frameworks

| # | Category | Question | Answer Framework |
|---|----------|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | General | What is digital marketing? | Define + list 7 pillars + why it matters for Indian businesses |
| 2 | General | Difference between SEO and SEM? | SEO = organic/free, SEM = paid + organic, give examples |
| 3 | General | How would you increase website traffic? | SEO (long-term) + paid ads (short-term) + content + social media |
| 4 | General | What is a landing page? | Single-purpose conversion page + 5 key elements |
| 5 | General | Explain the marketing funnel. | TOFU (awareness) → MOFU (consideration) → BOFU (decision) |
| 6 | SEO | Types of SEO? | On-page, off-page, technical, local — one example each |
| 7 | SEO | What is a backlink? | Link from another site = vote of trust → helps rankings |
| 8 | SEO | How do you do keyword research? | Seed keyword → tools (KW Planner, Ubersuggest) → filter by volume/difficulty/intent |
| 9 | SEO | Dofollow vs nofollow? | Dofollow passes link equity, nofollow does not |
| 10 | SEO | What are Core Web Vitals? | LCP, INP, CLS — explain each + why Google cares |
| 11 | Paid Ads | How does Google Ads bidding work? | Auction: bid + Quality Score = Ad Rank |
| 12 | Paid Ads | What is Quality Score? | 1-10 rating: CTR, ad relevance, landing page experience |
| 13 | Paid Ads | What is ROAS? | Revenue / Ad Spend. ₹5L revenue from ₹1L spend = 5x ROAS |
| 14 | Paid Ads | How to reduce cost per lead? | Better targeting, test creatives, optimize landing page, negative keywords |
| 15 | Paid Ads | What is retargeting? | Showing ads to people who visited but did not convert |
| 16 | Social | How to create a social media strategy? | Goals → audience → platforms → content pillars → schedule → metrics |
| 17 | Social | Best platform for B2B? | LinkedIn (primary) + YouTube (secondary) — explain why |
| 18 | Social | How to measure social media success? | Engagement rate, reach, website clicks, leads — not just likes |
| 19 | Social | Handling a negative viral post? | Acknowledge fast, respond transparently, do not delete |
| 20 | Social | How often to post on Instagram? | 4-5 Reels/week, 2-3 carousels/week, daily stories |
| 21 | Analytics | What is a UTM parameter? | URL tags tracking source/medium/campaign in GA4 |
| 22 | Analytics | Sessions vs users in GA4? | Users = unique people, Sessions = visits (one user, multiple sessions) |
| 23 | Strategy | Allocating ₹1L monthly budget? | 70-20-10 framework with channel percentages by goal |
| 24 | Analytics | What is conversion rate? | (Conversions / Visitors) x 100. Example: 50/2000 = 2.5% |
| 25 | Analytics | What is A/B testing? | Two versions, one variable, statistical significance |
| 26 | AI | How do you use AI in marketing? | Content drafting, keyword research, ad copy, data analysis |
| 27 | AI | Will AI replace marketers? | No — AI replaces tasks, not roles. Strategy and judgment stay human |
| 28 | Trends | Biggest DM trend right now? | AI campaigns, short-form video, conversational commerce |
| 29 | Trends | How to stay updated? | SEJ, Social Media Examiner, podcasts, LinkedIn creators |
| 30 | Career | Where do you see yourself in 3 years? | Specific role → skill depth → contribution → continuous learning |

## Freelancing Path

### Getting Started on Freelance Platforms

| Platform | Best For | Commission | Starting Strategy |
|----------|----------|-----------|------------------|
| **Fiverr** | Small tasks, beginners | 20% | Create 5 gigs with clear deliverables, competitive pricing |
| **Upwork** | Long-term projects, higher budgets | 10-20% | Write a strong proposal for every job, start with lower rates |
| **LinkedIn** | High-value clients, retainers | 0% | Post your work, engage with founders, send personalized pitches |
| **Freelancer.com** | Contests, one-off projects | 10% | Enter contests to build a portfolio |

### How to Get Your First 3 Clients

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | **Free work** — Offer to do SEO audit or social media review for 3 local businesses for free | Week 1-2 |
| 2 | **Case studies** — Document the results and create before/after case studies | Week 3 |
| 3 | **Referrals** — Ask those 3 businesses to refer you (offer them a discount on future work) | Week 4 |
| 4 | **Platform profiles** — Set up Fiverr + Upwork with your case studies as portfolio | Week 4 |
| 5 | **Outreach** — Send 10 personalized pitches per day to businesses that need marketing help | Week 5+ |
