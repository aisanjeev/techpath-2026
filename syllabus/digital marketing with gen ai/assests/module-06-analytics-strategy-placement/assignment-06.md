## Task 1: Set Up GA4 and Build a Marketing Dashboard (30 Marks)

### Objective
Implement Google Analytics 4 on a website, create custom explorations, and build a professional Looker Studio dashboard.

### What to Do

**Part A: GA4 Setup via Google Tag Manager (10 marks)**

1. Create a GA4 property (use the Google Merchandise Store demo account if you don't have a live website: https://analytics.google.com/analytics/web/demoAccount)
2. Set up Google Tag Manager (GTM) container
3. Create a GA4 Configuration tag in GTM
4. Configure Enhanced Measurement events:
   - Page views (automatic)
   - Scroll tracking (at 90% depth)
   - Outbound link clicks
   - File downloads
5. Create at least 2 custom events:
   - `cta_click` — fires when any CTA button is clicked
   - `form_start` — fires when a user begins filling a form
6. Mark at least 1 event as a Key Event (conversion)
7. Preview and test using GTM's Preview mode
8. Publish the GTM container

**Part B: Custom Explorations (10 marks)**

Create 3 explorations in GA4:

1. **Free Form Exploration:**
   - Rows: Page path
   - Columns: Device category
   - Values: Sessions, Engagement rate, Conversions
   - Filter: Last 28 days
   - Include at least 15 pages

2. **Funnel Exploration:**
   - Step 1: Session start
   - Step 2: Page view (specific product/service page)
   - Step 3: CTA click or form start
   - Step 4: Form submit or purchase
   - Show breakdown by traffic source

3. **Path Exploration:**
   - Starting point: Landing page (home page)
   - Show the top 5 paths users take after landing
   - Identify where users drop off

**Part C: Looker Studio Dashboard (10 marks)**

Build a 1-page marketing dashboard in Looker Studio:
- Connect to GA4 as data source
- Include these visualizations:
  - Scorecard row: Total Users, Sessions, Engagement Rate, Conversions (with comparison to previous period)
  - Line chart: Daily sessions trend (last 30 days)
  - Pie chart: Traffic sources breakdown
  - Table: Top 10 landing pages with sessions, bounce rate, conversions
  - Bar chart: Device category comparison
- Add a date range filter control
- Use clean design with proper colors and labels
- Add your name and "TechPath Academy" branding

### Submission:
- Screenshots of GTM setup (tags, triggers, variables)
- Screenshots of 3 Explorations
- Shareable link to Looker Studio dashboard
- Brief write-up (200 words): Key insights from the data

### Grading Criteria
| Criteria | Marks |
|----------|-------|
| GA4 + GTM setup correct (tags firing, events tracked) | 5 |
| Custom events configured and tested | 5 |
| Free Form Exploration (correct dimensions, metrics) | 3 |
| Funnel Exploration (logical steps, useful insights) | 4 |
| Path Exploration (starting point correct, insights noted) | 3 |
| Dashboard design (clean, all elements present) | 5 |
| Dashboard functionality (filters work, data accurate) | 3 |
| Write-up quality (actual insights, not just descriptions) | 2 |

## Task 2: Digital Marketing Strategy Proposal for a Real Local Business (30 Marks)

### Objective
Write a complete, professional digital marketing strategy proposal for a real local business in your city. This should be client-ready quality.

### What to Do

**Choose a Real Business:**
Pick any local business in your city (restaurant, gym, tuition centre, boutique, salon, clinic, etc.). Get their permission if possible, or use publicly available information.

**Your Proposal Must Include:**

**1. Business Audit (5 marks)**
- Current online presence assessment (website, social media, Google Business)
- Screenshot evidence of current state
- What's working and what's not
- Competitor analysis (3 competitors — their strengths, weaknesses, online strategy)

**2. Goals & KPIs (5 marks)**
- 3-5 SMART goals for the next 6 months
- KPIs for each goal (specific numbers)
- How you'll measure each KPI (which tool/report)

**3. Target Audience & Personas (4 marks)**
- 2 detailed buyer personas (name, age, occupation, pain points, online behavior, where they spend time)
- Customer journey map for each persona

**4. Channel Mix & Budget Allocation (6 marks)**
- Which channels to focus on and WHY (justify each choice)
- Monthly budget: Rs 5,00,000 annual (Rs 41,667/month) — break down by channel
- Expected ROI per channel
- Show monthly and quarterly allocation

**5. 90-Day Tactical Plan (6 marks)**
- Month 1: Setup and quick wins (what to do each week)
- Month 2: Scale and optimize
- Month 3: Review and expand
- Include a content calendar for the first month (specific post ideas)
- Ad campaign structure (campaigns, ad sets, ads)

**6. Measurement & Reporting (4 marks)**
- Weekly metrics to track
- Monthly reporting format (what the client sees)
- Decision criteria: When to increase budget, when to pause a channel
- Reporting tools you'll use

### Format Requirements:
- Professional document (Google Docs or PDF)
- 10-15 pages including visuals
- Include charts, tables, and mockups where helpful
- Use professional language (this should be sendable to a real client)
- Include your name, date, and "Prepared by [Name], TechPath Academy Graduate"

### Grading Criteria
| Criteria | Marks |
|----------|-------|
| Business audit (thorough, evidence-based) | 5 |
| Goals (SMART, realistic, measurable) | 5 |
| Audience personas (detailed, research-based) | 4 |
| Channel strategy (justified, budget allocated) | 6 |
| 90-day plan (specific, actionable, realistic) | 6 |
| Measurement plan (clear, useful) | 4 |

## Task 3: ATS-Optimized Resume + LinkedIn Profile (20 Marks)

### Objective
Create a professional, ATS-friendly resume and fully optimize your LinkedIn profile for digital marketing roles.

### What to Do

**Part A: Resume (10 marks)**

Create a 1-page resume optimized for ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems):

**Must Include:**
- Professional summary (3 lines — who you are, what you do, key achievement)
- Skills section with relevant keywords: Digital Marketing, SEO, Google Analytics, Social Media Marketing, Content Strategy, Email Marketing, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Marketing Automation, CRM, Data Analysis
- Education (include TechPath Academy Digital Marketing Program)
- Projects/Experience:
  - At least 3 projects from this course (present them professionally)
  - Use STAR format: Situation, Task, Action, Result
  - Include numbers wherever possible
- Certifications (list all Google/HubSpot/Meta certs you earned)
- Tools: List all tools you can use (GA4, Looker Studio, MailerLite, HubSpot, Zapier, Canva, etc.)

**Resume Rules:**
- Simple format — no tables, columns, graphics, or fancy fonts
- Use standard headings
- PDF format
- Action verbs to start each bullet (Managed, Created, Analyzed, Increased, Built, Optimized)
- Keep to 1 page

**Part B: LinkedIn Profile (10 marks)**

Optimize every section of your LinkedIn profile:

1. **Photo** — Professional headshot (plain background, good lighting)
2. **Banner** — Custom banner mentioning your specialty
3. **Headline** — Not just "Student" — use: "Digital Marketing Specialist | SEO | Paid Ads | Content Strategy | Helping Brands Grow Online"
4. **About** — 3-paragraph summary: Who you are, what you do, what you're looking for. Include keywords.
5. **Experience** — Add TechPath internship/projects as experience entries
6. **Education** — Add TechPath Academy with course details
7. **Skills** — Add 15+ relevant skills, get endorsements from classmates
8. **Certifications** — Add all certificates earned
9. **Featured** — Add 2-3 project links or portfolio pieces
10. **Recommendations** — Get at least 3 peer recommendations from classmates

**Peer Review Requirement:**
Share your resume and LinkedIn profile with 3 classmates. Each must provide written feedback (2-3 points each). Include their feedback and your improvements in your submission.

### Submission:
- Resume as PDF
- LinkedIn profile URL
- 3 peer review feedback screenshots
- Brief note on what you changed based on feedback

### Grading Criteria
| Criteria | Marks |
|----------|-------|
| Resume format (ATS-friendly, clean, 1-page) | 3 |
| Resume content (keywords, achievements, numbers) | 4 |
| Resume projects (well-described, results shown) | 3 |
| LinkedIn headline + about section | 3 |
| LinkedIn completeness (all sections filled) | 3 |
| LinkedIn featured + certifications | 2 |

## Task 4: Launch Freelance Profiles + Cold Outreach (20 Marks)

### Objective
Set up professional freelance profiles and practice client outreach — the first steps to earning as a digital marketer.

### What to Do

**Part A: Fiverr Profile (6 marks)**

Create a Fiverr seller account and publish 3 gigs:

1. **Gig 1: SEO Audit** — "I will audit your website SEO and provide an action report"
2. **Gig 2: Social Media Management** — "I will manage your Instagram or Facebook for 30 days"
3. **Gig 3: Google Analytics Setup** — "I will set up GA4 and GTM on your website"

Each gig must have:
- Compelling title (use Fiverr best practices)
- Detailed description (what's included, process, deliverables)
- 3 pricing packages (Basic / Standard / Premium)
- Relevant tags
- A professional gig image (create in Canva)
- FAQ section (3 questions)

**Part B: Upwork Profile (6 marks)**

Create an Upwork freelancer profile:

1. Professional title: "Digital Marketing Specialist | SEO, Google Ads, Social Media"
2. Overview/bio (500+ words describing your skills, experience, and approach)
3. Skills added (at least 10 relevant skills)
4. Portfolio items (add 3 project samples from this course)
5. Set your hourly rate (research market rate for your experience level)
6. Complete profile strength to 100%

Publish 3 specialized profiles (Upwork allows multiple):
- SEO & Content Marketing
- Social Media Marketing
- Paid Advertising (Google + Meta)

**Part C: Cold Outreach (8 marks)**

Send 5 cold pitches to potential clients. You can use email, LinkedIn, or Instagram DMs.

**Requirements for each pitch:**
- Personalized (mention something specific about their business)
- Value-first approach (offer a free audit or insight)
- Clear CTA (suggest a specific next step)
- Professional and concise (under 150 words)

**Target these types of businesses:**
- Local businesses you can see need help online
- Instagram accounts with poor engagement
- Websites with obvious SEO issues
- Businesses without Google Business profiles

**Submit:**
- Screenshot of each pitch sent (blur personal details of recipient)
- Template you used (with personalization framework)
- Results: Did anyone respond? What did you learn?

| Criteria | Marks |

| Fiverr pricing & packages (competitive, logical) | 2 |
| Upwork profile (complete, professional, compelling) | 4 |
| Upwork portfolio items (relevant, well-presented) | 2 |
| Cold pitches (5 sent, personalized, value-first) | 5 |
| Pitch quality (professional, clear CTA) | 2 |
