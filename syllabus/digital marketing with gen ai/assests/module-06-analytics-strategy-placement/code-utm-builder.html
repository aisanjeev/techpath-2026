Naming Rules (Follow These Always)

  • Always use lowercase — facebook not Facebook or FACEBOOK
  • Use underscores for spaces — summer_sale not summer sale or summer-sale
  • Include dates in campaign names — launch_jul2026 not launch
  • Be specific with sources — instagram_story or instagram_reel not just social
  • No special characters — Avoid ? & # @ ! in UTM values
  • Keep it readable — Someone else should understand what the campaign is from the UTM name alone
  • Document in a spreadsheet — Maintain a master UTM tracking sheet so your team stays consistent
