Naming Convention Guide

Rules for consistent UTM naming:

  • Always use lowercase — UTMs are case-sensitive in GA4
  • Use hyphens instead of spaces or underscores
  • Keep a consistent format: campaign-type-month-year
  • Document all UTMs in a shared spreadsheet
  • Never use special characters or Indian rupee symbol in UTMs
  • Examples: utm_campaign=diwali-sale-oct-2025, utm_source=linkedin
