## Why Strategy Matters More Than Tactics

Most beginners jump straight into tactics — "Let me run Instagram ads!" or "I need to post Reels!" But tactics without strategy is like driving fast without knowing the destination. You burn fuel (budget) but reach nowhere.

Think of strategy as the GPS and tactics as the accelerator. The GPS decides where you are going and which route to take. The accelerator decides how fast. Without the GPS, pressing the accelerator harder just gets you lost faster.

> **Real Example:** Mamaearth spent over ₹600 crore on marketing in one year, but their customer acquisition cost kept rising because competitors copied their tactics. Boat, on the other hand, built a clear strategy — partner with Indian cricketers, target the youth segment, dominate the ₹1,000-3,000 audio price range — and became the #1 audio brand in India with a fraction of the spend.

## The 7-Step Digital Marketing Strategy Framework

### Step 1: Audit the Current State

Before building anything new, understand where you stand today.

**SWOT Analysis:**

| | Helpful | Harmful |
|---|---------|---------|
| **Internal** | **Strengths:** What you do well (strong product, loyal customers, skilled team) | **Weaknesses:** Where you lag (no social presence, slow website, no email list) |
| **External** | **Opportunities:** Market trends in your favor (growing demand, competitor weakness, new platform) | **Threats:** Risks outside your control (new competitor, algorithm change, regulation) |

**Competitor Analysis Checklist:**
- What keywords do they rank for? (Check SEMrush or Ubersuggest)
- What social platforms are they active on? How many followers?
- What type of content do they post? How often?
- Are they running ads? What do the ads look like? (Check Meta Ad Library)
- What is their pricing? How do they position themselves?
- Read their Google reviews — what do customers complain about?

### Step 2: Define Clear Objectives

Vague goals like "grow the business" are useless. Use SMART goals and OKRs.

**SMART Goals:**

| Letter | Meaning | Bad Goal | SMART Goal |
|--------|---------|----------|------------|
| **S** | Specific | "Get more traffic" | "Increase organic traffic to the blog" |
| **M** | Measurable | "Improve social media" | "Grow Instagram followers from 5K to 15K" |
| **A** | Achievable | "Get 1 million users in a month" | "Get 5,000 new users in Q3" |
| **R** | Relevant | "Start a podcast" (for a hardware shop) | "Launch YouTube product reviews" |
| **T** | Time-bound | "Someday increase sales" | "Increase online sales by 30% by December 2026" |

**OKR Format (Objective + Key Results):**
- **Objective:** Become the go-to digital marketing training brand in South India
- **KR1:** Increase website organic traffic from 10K to 30K monthly sessions by Q4
- **KR2:** Generate 500 qualified leads per month through content marketing
- **KR3:** Achieve 4.8+ rating on Google Reviews with 200+ reviews

### Step 3: Identify Your Target Audience

**Customer Persona Template:**

| Field | Example |
|-------|---------|
| Name | "Career-Switcher Kavitha" |
| Age | 25-32 |
| Location | Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune |
| Education | BCA/BSc/BCom graduate |
| Current Role | Administrative job or fresher |
| Income | ₹3-6 LPA |
| Goal | Switch to a digital marketing career |
| Pain Point | No practical skills, confused by too many courses online |
| Preferred Platforms | Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp |
| Decision Trigger | Placement support, EMI option, success stories |

**TAM, SAM, SOM:**

| Term | Full Form | Meaning | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|---------|
| **TAM** | Total Addressable Market | Everyone who could theoretically buy | All 25-35 year olds in India wanting career training = 5 crore |
| **SAM** | Serviceable Addressable Market | The portion you can realistically reach | Those in tier-1/2 cities, English-speaking, online = 50 lakh |
| **SOM** | Serviceable Obtainable Market | What you can realistically capture in 1-2 years | 5,000 students (0.1% of SAM) |

### Step 4: Choose Your Channels

| Channel Type | What It Means | Examples | Best For |
|-------------|--------------|---------|----------|
| **Owned Media** | Channels you fully control | Website, blog, email list, app | Long-term authority, SEO, nurturing |
| **Earned Media** | Attention you earn (not pay for) | Press coverage, reviews, shares, backlinks | Trust, credibility, social proof |
| **Paid Media** | Channels you pay to access | Google Ads, Instagram Ads, Influencer partnerships | Quick reach, lead generation, launches |

**Channel selection framework:** Choose 2-3 channels maximum. Master them before adding more.

### Step 5: Create a Content Plan

Map content types to the customer journey stage:

| Stage | Customer Thinking | Content Type | Example |
|-------|------------------|-------------|---------|
| **Awareness** | "I have a problem" | Blog, social post, video | "Why Your Resume Gets Rejected by ATS" |
| **Consideration** | "What are the options?" | Comparison, webinar, case study | "TechPath vs Other Institutes — Honest Comparison" |
| **Decision** | "Should I buy this?" | Testimonial, demo, pricing page | "How Priya Got a ₹6 LPA Job After TechPath" |
| **Retention** | "Was this worth it?" | Email, community, support | Alumni WhatsApp group, monthly tips |

### Step 6: Set the Budget

(Covered in detail in the Budget Allocation section below)

### Step 7: Define KPIs and Measurement

| Goal | KPI | Target | Tool |
|------|-----|--------|------|
| Brand Awareness | Website traffic, social followers, impressions | 30K sessions/month | GA4, Instagram Insights |
| Lead Generation | Form submissions, cost per lead | 500 leads/month at ₹200/lead | CRM, Google Ads |
| Sales | Revenue, conversion rate, ROAS | ₹10L/month revenue, 3x ROAS | GA4 + CRM |
| Retention | NPS, churn rate, repeat purchase rate | NPS 50+, churn <5% | Survey tool, CRM |

## Attribution Models

Attribution answers: "Which touchpoint gets credit for the conversion?" A customer might see your Instagram ad, read your blog, click an email, and then buy. Who gets the credit?

| Model | How Credit is Assigned | Best For |
|-------|----------------------|----------|
| **First Click** | 100% credit to the first touchpoint | Understanding what drives awareness |
| **Last Click** | 100% credit to the last touchpoint before conversion | Simple businesses, small budgets |
| **Linear** | Equal credit to all touchpoints | Balanced view across the funnel |
| **Time Decay** | More credit to touchpoints closer to conversion | Long sales cycles (B2B, real estate) |
| **Position-Based (U-shaped)** | 40% first, 40% last, 20% split among middle | Most common balanced model |
| **Data-Driven** | AI assigns credit based on actual data patterns | Large businesses with lots of data |

**Example journey:**

```
Instagram Ad → Blog Visit → Email Click → Google Search → Purchase (₹5,000)
```

| Model | Instagram | Blog | Email | Google |
|-------|-----------|------|-------|--------|
| First Click | ₹5,000 | ₹0 | ₹0 | ₹0 |
| Last Click | ₹0 | ₹0 | ₹0 | ₹5,000 |
| Linear | ₹1,250 | ₹1,250 | ₹1,250 | ₹1,250 |
| Position-Based | ₹2,000 | ₹500 | ₹500 | ₹2,000 |

> **Pro Tip:** If you are a small business spending under ₹5 lakh/month on marketing, use Last Click attribution. It is the simplest and gives you the most actionable data. Switch to Position-Based when your budget grows and you have multiple active channels.

## Budget Allocation Frameworks

### The 70-20-10 Rule

| Category | % of Budget | What It Means | Example (₹2L Budget) |
|----------|-------------|--------------|----------------------|
| **Proven (70%)** | 70% | Channels you know work well for you | ₹1,40,000 on Google Ads (proven ROI) |
| **Promising (20%)** | 20% | Channels showing early positive signals | ₹40,000 on Instagram Reels ads (growing, needs more testing) |
| **Experimental (10%)** | 10% | New channels or creative ideas | ₹20,000 on a micro-influencer campaign (untested) |

### Budget by Business Stage

| Stage | Monthly Budget Range | Focus |
|-------|---------------------|-------|
| **Startup (0-1 year)** | ₹50,000 - ₹2,00,000 | Brand awareness, lead gen, content creation |
| **Growth (1-3 years)** | ₹2,00,000 - ₹10,00,000 | Scale proven channels, add new channels, hire specialists |
| **Mature (3+ years)** | ₹10,00,000+ | Market leadership, brand campaigns, multi-channel |

### Channel Allocation by Business Type

| Business Type | SEO/Content | Paid Ads | Social Media | Email/WhatsApp |
|--------------|------------|----------|-------------|---------------|
| E-commerce (D2C) | 20% | 40% | 25% | 15% |
| SaaS/Tech | 30% | 30% | 15% | 25% |
| Local Business | 25% | 25% | 30% | 20% |
| Education/Coaching | 25% | 30% | 25% | 20% |
| B2B Services | 30% | 20% | 20% | 30% |

## Forecasting with Funnel Math

You can predict revenue by working backwards from your conversion funnel.

**Example: Digital marketing course selling for ₹25,000**

| Funnel Stage | Numbers | Conversion Rate |
|-------------|---------|----------------|
| Website Visitors | 10,000 | — |
| Lead Magnet Downloads | 1,500 | 15% of visitors |
| Free Webinar Attendees | 300 | 20% of downloads |
| Sales Page Visitors | 150 | 50% of webinar attendees |
| Purchases | 30 | 20% of sales page visitors |
| **Revenue** | **₹7,50,000** | 30 x ₹25,000 |

If you need ₹15 lakh revenue → you need 60 purchases → 600 webinar attendees → 3,000 downloads → 20,000 visitors.

Now you know exactly how much traffic to target.

## Building a Marketing Pitch Deck

Whether pitching to a client, your boss, or an investor, every marketing pitch follows this structure:

| Slide | Content | Time |
|-------|---------|------|
| 1. **Problem** | What pain exists in the market? | 1 min |
| 2. **Solution** | How your marketing strategy solves it | 1 min |
| 3. **Market** | TAM/SAM/SOM, target audience persona | 1 min |
| 4. **Strategy** | Channels, content plan, timeline | 2 min |
| 5. **Traction** | Results so far (traffic, leads, sales) | 1 min |
| 6. **Team** | Who is executing? What expertise? | 30 sec |
| 7. **Budget & Ask** | How much you need and expected ROI | 1 min |

## Pricing Models for Agencies and Freelancers

| Model | How It Works | Typical Indian Rates | Best For |
|-------|-------------|---------------------|----------|
| **Monthly Retainer** | Fixed monthly fee for ongoing services | ₹25,000 - ₹2,00,000/month | Long-term clients, predictable income |
| **Project-Based** | One-time fee for a defined scope | ₹50,000 - ₹5,00,000 per project | Website builds, campaign launches |
| **Hourly** | Bill by the hour | ₹500 - ₹3,000/hour | Consulting, audits, training |
| **Performance-Based** | Fee tied to results (leads, sales) | % of ad spend or per-lead fee | Confident agencies, measurable outcomes |
| **Hybrid** | Base retainer + performance bonus | ₹15,000 base + ₹200/lead | Balanced risk sharing |

> **Pro Tip:** As a fresher freelancer, start with project-based pricing. It is easiest to sell because the client knows exactly what they are paying and what they get. Shift to retainers once you have proven your value over 2-3 months.

## Trainer Activity: Create a 3-Month Marketing Plan

**Time:** 20 minutes

**Scenario:** You are hired as a digital marketing consultant for a new co-working space in Pune. They have a monthly budget of ₹2,00,000 for the first 3 months.

**Create a marketing plan covering:**

1. **SWOT Analysis** — 2 items in each quadrant
2. **SMART Goal** — One specific, measurable goal for the 3 months
3. **Target Persona** — Age, profession, location, pain point
4. **Channel Selection** — Pick 3 channels with budget allocation (must add up to ₹2,00,000/month)
5. **Month-by-Month Breakdown:**
   - Month 1: Focus on ____________
   - Month 2: Focus on ____________
   - Month 3: Focus on ____________
6. **KPIs** — 3 metrics you will track and their targets

Present the plan in 3 minutes to the class. Classmates vote on the most convincing plan.

## Summary

- **Strategy before tactics** — know your destination before pressing the accelerator
- The 7-step framework: **Audit → Objectives → Audience → Channels → Content → Budget → KPIs**
- Use **SMART goals** and **OKRs** to set clear, measurable objectives
- Build **customer personas** with real demographics, pain points, and behavior
- **TAM/SAM/SOM** helps size your market from total opportunity to realistic capture
- **Attribution models** decide which marketing touchpoint gets credit for the sale
- Use **Last Click** for small budgets, **Position-Based** when you have multiple channels
- **70-20-10 rule**: 70% on proven channels, 20% on promising, 10% on experimental
- Startup budgets range from **₹50K-2L/month**; growth budgets from **₹2L-10L/month**
- **Funnel math** lets you forecast revenue by working backwards from conversion rates
- Freelancer pricing models: **retainer, project-based, hourly, or performance-based**
