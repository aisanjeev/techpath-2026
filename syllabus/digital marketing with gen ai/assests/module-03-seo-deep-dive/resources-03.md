## Practice Websites for SEO Audits

Use these types of sites to practice your SEO audit skills:
- Your own blog or portfolio site
- Local business websites in your city (restaurants, coaching classes, gyms)
- Small e-commerce sites
- College/university websites
- NGO/nonprofit websites

**Note:** Always ask permission before sharing audit results publicly or contacting website owners with recommendations.
