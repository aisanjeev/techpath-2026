Keyword Selection Criteria Guide

Volume Sweet Spot

For new blogs, target 100-1,000 monthly searches. High enough for traffic, low enough to rank.

KD Under 40

Keywords with difficulty below 40 are achievable without massive backlink profiles. Below 20 = easy wins.

Intent Match

Blog posts work best with Informational intent. Commercial intent keywords suit comparison/review posts.

CPC as Value Signal

Higher CPC means advertisers pay more — the keyword has commercial value even for organic content.

Long-tail Advantage

3-5 word phrases are easier to rank for, have clearer intent, and convert better than head terms.

Relevance First

A perfect-metric keyword is worthless if it doesn't match your content. Relevance trumps numbers.
