## Part 1: YouTube Advertising

YouTube is the world's second-largest search engine and India's most-watched video platform. With 500+ million monthly active users in India, YouTube ads offer massive reach at very low costs.

> **Fun Fact:** Indians watch more YouTube per capita than any other country. The average Indian user spends 40+ minutes on YouTube daily. For advertisers, this means your audience is already there — you just need to reach them with the right ad format.

---

## YouTube Ad Formats

| Format | Duration | Skippable? | When User Pays | Best For |
|--------|----------|-----------|----------------|----------|
| **TrueView In-Stream (Skippable)** | Any length (15s-3min recommended) | Yes, after 5 seconds | You pay only when viewer watches 30s or the full ad (whichever is shorter), or clicks | Product demos, testimonials, brand stories |
| **TrueView Discovery** | Any length | N/A (user chooses to click) | You pay when user clicks to watch | Educational content, how-to videos, long-form content |
| **Bumper Ads** | 6 seconds max | No, cannot skip | You pay per 1,000 impressions (CPM) | Quick brand recall, offers, taglines |
| **Non-Skippable In-Stream** | 15 seconds max | No, cannot skip | You pay per 1,000 impressions (CPM) | Important announcements, high-impact messages |
| **Masthead** | Full day | N/A (homepage takeover) | Fixed daily rate (₹50 lakh+) | Major launches, national campaigns (enterprise only) |

### How Each Format Appears

```
TrueView In-Stream:
┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│  YOUR AD VIDEO                    │
│  [Skip Ad in 5s →]               │
│                                   │
│  "Learn Digital Marketing in     │
│   6 months. TechPath Academy."   │
│                                   │
│  [Visit Site]  [Learn More]      │
└──────────────────────────────────┘
→ After 5 seconds: [Skip Ad] button appears
→ You only pay if they watch 30+ seconds or click

Bumper Ad:
┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│  6-SECOND VIDEO                   │
│  No skip button                   │
│  "TechPath. Learn. Build. Grow." │
│  Ad ends automatically            │
└──────────────────────────────────┘
→ You pay per 1,000 views (CPM)

TrueView Discovery:
Appears in YouTube search results and "Up Next" sidebar
┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│  [Thumbnail Image] AD            │
│  "How to Start Digital Marketing │
│   Career in India | Full Guide"  │
│  TechPath Academy • 250K views   │
└──────────────────────────────────┘
→ You pay only when someone clicks to watch
```

> **Pro Tip:** TrueView In-Stream is the most cost-effective format for Indian advertisers. You pay only when someone actually watches 30 seconds of your ad. If they skip at 5 seconds, you pay nothing. The average cost per view in India is just ₹1-5.

## YouTube Targeting Options

| Targeting Type | How It Works | Example |
|---------------|-------------|---------|
| **Keywords** | Show ads on videos related to specific keywords | "digital marketing tutorial", "learn SEO" |
| **Topics** | Show ads on videos about specific topics | "Education > Computer Training" |
| **Placements** | Choose specific YouTube channels or videos | Show ads only on a popular tech channel |
| **Custom Intent** | Target people who recently searched on Google for specific terms | People who searched "digital marketing course" on Google see your YouTube ad |
| **Affinity Audiences** | Long-term interests and lifestyle | "Technology Enthusiasts", "Job Seekers" |
| **In-Market Audiences** | People actively researching or shopping | "Education > Computer Training" |
| **Demographics** | Age, gender, household income, parental status | 20-35 years old, all genders |
| **Life Events** | Major life changes | "Starting a new job", "Graduating college" |
| **Remarketing** | People who visited your website or watched your previous videos | Show ad to people who visited your course page but did not enquire |

> **Real Example:** TechPath could target people in Madhya Pradesh aged 18-30 who recently searched "digital marketing career" on Google (Custom Intent) and show them a 60-second testimonial video from a placed student. This combines Google's search data with YouTube's video power.

## Video Ad Creative Tips

### The 5-Second Rule

The first 5 seconds determine everything. If you do not hook the viewer in 5 seconds, they hit "Skip Ad."

| Do (First 5 Seconds) | Do Not |
|----------------------|--------|
| Start with a bold question: "Want to earn ₹50,000/month in digital marketing?" | Start with your logo animation |
| Show a surprising visual or number | Start with "Hi, my name is..." |
| Address a pain point: "Tired of getting rejected in interviews?" | Start with slow background music |
| Use a person talking directly to camera | Show a generic stock video |

### Other Creative Tips

| Tip | Why It Works |
|-----|-------------|
| **Mobile-first framing** | 70%+ YouTube views in India are on mobile — use vertical (9:16) or square (1:1) for Stories/Shorts, landscape (16:9) for In-Stream |
| **Add subtitles/captions** | Many Indians watch videos without sound (public transport, office, etc.) |
| **Include a clear CTA** | Tell viewers exactly what to do: "Click the link below", "Visit techpath.biz" |
| **Show real people** | Students, trainers, campus footage — authenticity beats production quality |
| **Keep it under 60 seconds** | For awareness ads, 15-30s is ideal. For tutorials/testimonials, 60-90s max |
| **End with branding** | Show logo, website, and phone number in the last 3 seconds |

## YouTube Campaign Setup — Step by Step

1. **Go to Google Ads** (ads.google.com) — YouTube ads are managed through Google Ads
2. **Create New Campaign** > Choose objective (Awareness, Consideration, or Conversions)
3. **Select "Video" campaign type**
4. **Choose ad format** (In-Stream, Bumper, Discovery, etc.)
5. **Set budget and bidding**:
   - Daily budget (start with ₹500-1,000/day for testing)
   - Bidding: Maximum CPV (cost per view) for awareness, Target CPA for conversions
6. **Set targeting**:
   - Location (e.g., Madhya Pradesh or all India)
   - Language (Hindi, English)
   - Audiences (Custom Intent, In-Market, Affinity, or Remarketing)
7. **Upload your video** (must already be uploaded to YouTube)
8. **Add companion assets**: Display URL, CTA button text, sitelinks
9. **Review and launch**

## Part 2: LinkedIn Advertising

LinkedIn is the world's largest professional network with 130+ million users in India. It is the only platform where you can target people by their **job title, company, industry, and seniority level**.

> **Important Reality Check:** LinkedIn is the most expensive ad platform. CPC starts at ₹150 and can go up to ₹500+. But for B2B businesses, recruitment, and high-ticket services (MBA programs, enterprise software, consulting), LinkedIn's precision targeting makes the high cost worthwhile.

---

## LinkedIn Ad Formats

| Format | Where It Appears | Best For | Specs |
|--------|-----------------|----------|-------|
| **Sponsored Content** | LinkedIn feed (looks like a regular post) | Thought leadership, case studies, blog promotion | Image: 1200x627, Video: up to 10 min, Carousel: 2-10 cards |
| **Message Ads (InMail)** | Direct message inbox | Event invitations, personalised offers, B2B outreach | Subject line + body text + CTA button |
| **Text Ads** | Right sidebar and top banner (desktop only) | Low-budget brand awareness | 25-char headline + 75-char description + 50x50 image |
| **Dynamic Ads** | Right sidebar, personalised with user's name/photo | Job postings, company page followers | Automatically personalised |
| **Conversation Ads** | Message inbox with multiple CTA buttons | Multiple-choice engagement, event registrations | Choose-your-own-adventure style |

### Sponsored Content Sub-types

| Type | Format | Best For |
|------|--------|---------|
| **Single Image Ad** | One image + text | Blog posts, infographics, announcements |
| **Video Ad** | Video + text | Brand stories, product demos, testimonials |
| **Carousel Ad** | 2-10 swipeable cards | Multiple offerings, step-by-step stories |
| **Document Ad** | Swipeable PDF/document preview | Whitepapers, case studies, guides (users can read without leaving LinkedIn) |
| **Event Ad** | Promote a LinkedIn Event | Webinars, workshops, conferences |

---

## LinkedIn Targeting

LinkedIn's targeting is unmatched for B2B. No other platform lets you target by job title and company name.

| Targeting Type | Options | Example |
|---------------|---------|---------|
| **Job Title** | Specific titles | "Marketing Manager", "CTO", "HR Director" |
| **Company Name** | Specific companies | "TCS", "Infosys", "Wipro" |
| **Company Size** | Employee count | "51-200", "1,001-5,000" |
| **Industry** | Business sector | "Information Technology", "Education Management" |
| **Seniority** | Level in organisation | "Entry", "Senior", "VP", "C-Suite" |
| **Skills** | Skills listed on profile | "Digital Marketing", "Python", "Data Analysis" |
| **Groups** | LinkedIn Group membership | "Digital Marketing Professionals India" |
| **Education** | Degree, field of study, school | "MBA", "IIM Ahmedabad" |
| **Years of Experience** | Total work experience | "3-5 years", "10+ years" |
| **Location** | Country, state, city | "Mumbai", "Bengaluru", "Delhi NCR" |

> **Real Example:** A B2B SaaS company selling HR software could target: Job Title = "HR Director" or "CHRO" + Company Size = 500+ employees + Industry = "IT Services" + Location = India. This audience would be around 5,000-15,000 professionals — extremely precise.

## LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms

One of LinkedIn's most powerful features. Lead Gen Forms are **pre-filled forms** that auto-populate with the user's LinkedIn profile data (name, email, job title, company). The user just clicks "Submit" — no typing required.

### How It Works

```
User sees your Sponsored Content in feed
→ Clicks CTA button ("Download Guide" or "Register Now")
→ Form opens IN LinkedIn (no website needed)
→ Fields are already filled from their profile:
   Name: Priya Sharma
   Email: priya.sharma@company.com
   Job Title: Marketing Manager
   Company: TCS
→ User clicks "Submit"
→ Lead delivered to your CRM
```

### Why Lead Gen Forms Work

| Advantage | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **No landing page needed** | Everything happens within LinkedIn — no website friction |
| **Pre-filled data** | Users do not need to type — just click Submit. Conversion rates are 2-3x higher |
| **Accurate data** | Information comes from LinkedIn profiles (verified professional emails, real job titles) |
| **Mobile optimised** | Works perfectly on phones (where 60%+ of LinkedIn traffic comes from) |

> **Pro Tip:** Indian professionals are very protective of their email/phone. LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms work better than custom landing pages because users feel safer sharing data within the LinkedIn app. Typical LinkedIn Lead Gen Form conversion rate in India: 10-15% (vs 2-5% on a regular landing page).

## LinkedIn Pricing Reality

| Metric | Average (India) | Compare with Meta |
|--------|----------------|------------------|
| **CPC (Cost Per Click)** | ₹150-500+ | ₹5-15 on Meta (10-30x cheaper) |
| **CPM (Cost Per 1,000 Impressions)** | ₹800-2,000 | ₹80-200 on Meta (10x cheaper) |
| **CPL (Cost Per Lead)** | ₹500-2,000 | ₹100-300 on Meta (5-7x cheaper) |
| **Minimum Daily Budget** | ₹800/day | ₹100/day on Meta |
| **Minimum CPC Bid** | ₹150 | No minimum on Meta |

> **When is the high cost justified?** If your product/service has a high lifetime value (LTV). A B2B software company selling ₹5,00,000/year subscriptions can afford ₹2,000 per lead if they close 1 in 20 leads. But a coaching institute selling ₹45,000 courses should use Meta Ads instead.

## When to Use LinkedIn vs Meta vs Google

| Factor | Google Ads | Meta Ads | LinkedIn Ads |
|--------|-----------|----------|-------------|
| **Best audience** | People searching for your product | General consumers based on interests | Working professionals, B2B buyers |
| **Intent** | High (they are searching) | Low-Medium (you are interrupting) | Medium (professional browsing) |
| **Cost (India)** | ₹15-80 CPC | ₹5-15 CPC | ₹150-500 CPC |
| **B2C products** | Excellent | Excellent | Poor (too expensive) |
| **B2B products** | Good (Search) | Medium | Excellent |
| **Lead quality** | High (intent-based) | Medium (interest-based) | Very High (professional profile data) |
| **Retargeting** | Good | Excellent | Limited |
| **Minimum budget** | ₹500/day | ₹100/day | ₹800/day |

### Decision Framework

| If your business is... | Use |
|----------------------|-----|
| Local service (salon, restaurant, coaching class) | Meta Ads + Google Search |
| E-commerce (products) | Google Shopping + Meta Ads |
| B2B SaaS / Enterprise | LinkedIn Ads + Google Search |
| Brand building (new product launch) | Meta Ads + YouTube |
| Recruiting / Hiring | LinkedIn Ads |
| High-ticket services (₹1 lakh+) | LinkedIn + Google Search |
| App promotion | Meta Ads + Google App campaigns |

## Summary

- **YouTube** is India's largest video platform with 500M+ users — ads cost just ₹1-5 per view
- Six YouTube ad formats: TrueView In-Stream (skippable, pay after 30s), Discovery (user clicks to watch), Bumper (6s, non-skip), Non-skippable (15s), Masthead (homepage takeover)
- Video ad rule: **hook in 5 seconds** or the viewer skips — start with a bold question, surprising number, or pain point
- YouTube targeting includes Custom Intent (targeting people based on their Google searches), which is uniquely powerful
- **LinkedIn** is the most expensive platform (CPC ₹150-500+) but offers unmatched professional targeting: job title, company, industry, seniority
- **LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms** auto-fill from user profiles and get 2-3x higher conversion rates than regular landing pages
- Use LinkedIn only for **B2B, high-ticket services, or recruitment** — it is too expensive for B2C coaching and consumer products
- **Budget allocation** depends on your business type: coaching institutes should prioritise Meta + Google Search; B2B companies should prioritise LinkedIn + Google Search
- Never spread your budget too thin — dominate 2-3 platforms before expanding to more
- Measure everything with conversion tracking — without it, you are guessing which platform delivers real ROI
