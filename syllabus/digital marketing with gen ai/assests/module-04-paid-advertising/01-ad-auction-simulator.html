Key Insights

Strategy: Quality Focus

Low bid (₹20) + High Quality Score (9) = Ad Rank 180. You pay less per click because Google rewards relevance. Best for long-term campaigns.

Strategy: Outbid Everyone

High bid (₹80) + Low Quality Score (4) = Ad Rank 320. You can win position but pay premium CPCs. Unsustainable for most budgets.

Strategy: Balanced

Medium bid (₹45) + Medium Quality Score (7) = Ad Rank 315. Good balance of competitiveness and cost efficiency. Works for most advertisers.
