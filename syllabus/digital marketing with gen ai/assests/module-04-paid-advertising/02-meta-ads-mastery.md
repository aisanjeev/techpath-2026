## Why Meta Ads?

Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram advertising) is the most popular paid advertising platform for Indian businesses. With 450+ million Facebook users and 350+ million Instagram users in India, it offers unmatched reach at affordable prices.

> **Real Example:** A small beauty salon in Jaipur spent ₹15,000/month on Meta ads targeting women aged 22-35 within a 10 km radius. They generated 85 leads per month at ₹176 per lead. Three months later, their monthly revenue increased by ₹2,00,000 from those leads alone.

## Facebook Business Manager Setup

**Meta Business Manager** (business.facebook.com) is the central hub for managing your Meta ads, pages, pixels, and team members.

### Setup Steps

1. Go to business.facebook.com
2. Click "Create Account"
3. Enter your business name, your name, and business email
4. Add your Facebook Page to Business Manager
5. Create or connect an Ad Account
6. Add team members with appropriate roles (Admin, Advertiser, Analyst)
7. Install Meta Pixel on your website
8. Set up payment method (credit/debit card or UPI in India)

### Ad Account Structure

```
Business Manager
├── Facebook Page (TechPath Academy)
├── Instagram Account (connected)
├── Ad Account (payment + campaigns)
├── Meta Pixel (website tracking)
├── Custom Audiences (retargeting lists)
├── Product Catalog (for shopping ads)
└── Team Members (with role-based access)
```

## Campaign Objectives

When creating a campaign, Meta asks you to choose an objective. This tells Meta's algorithm what result to optimise for.

| Objective | What It Optimises For | Best For | Typical Indian Cost |
|-----------|----------------------|----------|-------------------|
| **Awareness** | Maximum impressions/reach | New brand launch, event promotion | ₹50-150 per 1,000 impressions |
| **Traffic** | Link clicks to website | Blog posts, landing pages | ₹2-8 per click |
| **Engagement** | Likes, comments, shares | Building social proof, viral content | ₹1-5 per engagement |
| **Leads** | Form submissions (in-app lead form) | Coaching institutes, real estate, services | ₹50-300 per lead |
| **Sales** | Purchases on website | E-commerce, online courses | ₹200-1,000 per sale |
| **App Installs** | App downloads | Mobile apps | ₹20-80 per install |

> **Pro Tip:** Always match your objective to your actual goal. If you want leads but choose "Traffic", Meta will send people who click but never fill the form. Choose "Leads" and Meta will find people who are more likely to submit forms.

## Audience Types

Meta's biggest strength is its audience targeting. You can reach exactly the right people based on who they are, what they do, and how they behave.

### 1. Core Audiences (Manual Targeting)

You define the audience using demographics, interests, and behaviours.

| Targeting Type | Options | Example |
|---------------|---------|---------|
| **Location** | Country, state, city, pin code, radius around a point | "People within 15 km of Varanasi" |
| **Age** | 18-65+ | "25-40 years old" |
| **Gender** | Male, Female, All | "Female" |
| **Language** | Any language | "Hindi, English" |
| **Interests** | Based on pages liked, content engaged with | "Digital Marketing, Entrepreneurship, MBA" |
| **Behaviours** | Purchase behaviour, device usage, travel | "Smartphone users, Online shoppers" |
| **Education** | Level, school, field of study | "College graduates" |
| **Job Title** | (limited compared to LinkedIn) | "Marketing Manager, Business Owner" |

### 2. Custom Audiences (Retargeting)

Target people who have already interacted with your business.

| Source | What It Targets | Use Case |
|--------|----------------|----------|
| **Website visitors** | People who visited your site (via Meta Pixel) | Retarget visitors who did not enquire |
| **Email/phone list** | Upload your CRM contacts | Upsell existing customers |
| **Instagram engagers** | People who liked, commented, or saved your posts | Convert followers into leads |
| **Facebook Page fans** | People who interacted with your Page | Promote new courses to engaged followers |
| **Video viewers** | People who watched 25%, 50%, 75%, or 95% of your video | Target people who showed high interest |
| **Lead form openers** | People who opened but did not submit your lead form | Nudge them to complete the form |
| **App users** | People who installed or used your app | Drive app engagement |

> **Pro Tip:** The most powerful custom audience is "Website visitors in last 7 days who visited the course page but did not fill the enquiry form." This audience is warm — they already showed interest. A retargeting ad saying "Still thinking? Here is what 500 students achieved at TechPath" converts extremely well.

### 3. Lookalike Audiences

Meta finds new people who are similar to your existing customers.

| Seed Audience | Lookalike Size | What Meta Does |
|--------------|---------------|---------------|
| Your top 100 customers (email list) | 1% Lookalike (India) | Finds the 1% of India's population most similar to your customers |
| Website converters (people who enquired) | 2% Lookalike | Slightly broader, more reach, slightly less precise |
| Instagram engagers | 5% Lookalike | Very broad reach, useful for awareness |

| Lookalike % | Size (India) | Precision | Best For |
|-------------|-------------|-----------|---------|
| 1% | ~4.5 million people | Highest | Lead generation, sales |
| 2-3% | ~9-14 million | Medium | Scaling after 1% works |
| 5-10% | ~23-45 million | Lower | Brand awareness campaigns |

> **Rule:** Always start with 1% Lookalike. Only expand to 2-5% after the 1% audience is delivering profitable results.

## Ad Placements

Meta can show your ad in many different places across Facebook and Instagram.

| Placement | Where It Appears | Best For |
|-----------|-----------------|----------|
| **Facebook Feed** | Main scrolling feed | All objectives, highest reach |
| **Instagram Feed** | Main scrolling feed | Visual products, brand awareness |
| **Instagram Stories** | Full-screen vertical (disappears in 24h) | Offers, announcements, behind-the-scenes |
| **Instagram Reels** | Short-form video feed | Video ads, younger audiences |
| **Facebook Marketplace** | Buy/sell section | Local businesses, product sellers |
| **Facebook Right Column** | Desktop only, small ads on the right | Retargeting (cheap impressions) |
| **Audience Network** | Third-party apps and websites | Extending reach beyond Facebook/Instagram |
| **Messenger** | Facebook Messenger inbox | Direct conversations, customer service |

> **Pro Tip:** For beginners, use **Advantage+ Placements** (let Meta decide where to show your ad). Meta's algorithm is very good at finding where your ad performs best. Once you have data, you can manually exclude underperforming placements.

## Ad Formats

| Format | Description | Best For | Specs |
|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| **Single Image** | One image with text | Simple offers, announcements | 1080 x 1080 px (square) or 1200 x 628 px (landscape) |
| **Carousel** | 2-10 swipeable cards, each with its own image/link | Multiple products/features, storytelling | 1080 x 1080 px per card |
| **Video** | Up to 240 minutes (15-30s recommended) | Demos, testimonials, brand stories | 1080 x 1080 (feed), 1080 x 1920 (Stories/Reels) |
| **Collection** | Cover image/video + 4 product images below | E-commerce, catalogs | Automatic from Product Catalog |
| **Instant Experience** | Full-screen mobile landing page within Facebook | Immersive brand experience, product showcase | Built using Meta's template editor |

## Creative Best Practices

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Hook in first 3 seconds** | For video ads, the first 3 seconds determine if someone watches or scrolls past. Start with a bold statement, question, or visual surprise |
| **Text on image** | Keep text under 20% of the image area. Facebook does not ban text-heavy images anymore, but they perform worse |
| **Aspect ratios** | Feed: 1:1 (square) or 4:5 (vertical). Stories/Reels: 9:16 (full vertical). Landscape (16:9) wastes mobile screen space |
| **Mobile-first design** | 98% of Indian Facebook users are on mobile. Design for small screens first |
| **Clear CTA** | Every ad needs a clear call-to-action: "Enquire Now", "Download Free Guide", "Book Demo" |
| **Social proof** | Include student count, ratings, or testimonials: "Join 2,000+ students trained at TechPath" |
| **Local relevance** | Use city names, local landmarks, regional language phrases to connect with Indian audiences |
| **UGC-style content** | User-Generated Content style (filmed on phone, casual) outperforms polished studio content |

> **Fun Fact:** According to Meta's own data, video ads shot on a smartphone with natural lighting outperform professionally produced studio videos by 63% in engagement for Indian audiences. Authenticity beats perfection.

## Campaign Budget Optimisation (CBO) vs Ad Set Budget

| Feature | CBO (Campaign Budget) | ABO (Ad Set Budget) |
|---------|----------------------|-------------------|
| **Where budget is set** | Campaign level | Individual ad set level |
| **Budget distribution** | Meta automatically allocates budget to best-performing ad sets | You manually decide how much each ad set spends |
| **Best for** | When you trust Meta's algorithm, scaling campaigns | Testing phase, when you want equal budget per audience |
| **Control** | Less manual control | Full control over each audience's spend |

**Recommendation for beginners:**
1. Start with **ABO** (Ad Set Budget) during the testing phase — give each audience equal budget to see which performs best
2. Switch to **CBO** (Campaign Budget) once you have a winning audience — let Meta allocate more budget to the winner automatically

## A/B Testing Strategy

| What to Test | How to Test | Why |
|-------------|------------|-----|
| **Audiences** | Create 2-3 ad sets with different targeting, same creative | Find which audience converts best |
| **Creatives** | Same audience, 2-3 different ad images/videos | Find which visual drives most clicks/leads |
| **Ad copy** | Same image, different headlines and descriptions | Find which message resonates most |
| **Placements** | Feed only vs Stories only vs All placements | See where your audience engages most |
| **Objectives** | Traffic vs Leads vs Engagement | See which objective delivers best ROI |

**Testing rules:**
- Change only ONE variable at a time
- Give each test at least 3-5 days and ₹1,000-2,000 minimum budget
- Need at least 50 conversions to draw reliable conclusions
- Kill underperforming ads after 3 days if cost is 3x your target

## Indian Ad Benchmarks

| Metric | Average (India) | Good | Excellent |
|--------|----------------|------|-----------|
| **CPC (Cost Per Click)** | ₹5-15 | ₹3-8 | Below ₹3 |
| **CTR (Click-Through Rate)** | 1-3% | 3-5% | Above 5% |
| **CPL (Cost Per Lead)** | ₹100-300 | ₹50-100 | Below ₹50 |
| **CPM (Cost Per 1000 Impressions)** | ₹80-200 | ₹50-80 | Below ₹50 |
| **Conversion Rate (Landing Page)** | 2-5% | 5-10% | Above 10% |

> **Pro Tip:** Indian ad costs are significantly cheaper than US/UK markets (5-10x cheaper). This makes India one of the best markets for testing ad strategies with a small budget. Even ₹500/day can generate meaningful data.

## Scaling Strategies

Once you find a winning ad (good CTR, low CPL), here is how to scale:

| Strategy | How It Works | Risk Level |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Increase budget by 20%** every 2-3 days | Gradual scaling keeps the algorithm stable | Low |
| **Duplicate ad set** to a Lookalike audience | Same winning creative, new similar audience | Medium |
| **Expand geo-targeting** | Winning in Bhopal? Expand to all of MP, then all of India | Medium |
| **Add new creatives** to winning ad set | Prevent ad fatigue while keeping the proven audience | Low |
| **Switch to CBO** | Let Meta auto-distribute budget to winners | Low-Medium |

> **Important Warning:** Never increase your budget by more than 20-30% at once. A sudden jump (e.g., ₹500/day to ₹5,000/day) resets Meta's learning phase and can destroy your campaign performance.

## Summary

- **Meta Business Manager** is the central hub for all Facebook and Instagram advertising
- Choose the right **campaign objective** — Leads for enquiries, Traffic for website visits, Awareness for new brands
- Three audience types: **Core** (demographics + interests), **Custom** (retargeting past visitors), **Lookalike** (finding similar new people)
- Start with **1% Lookalike** audiences for best precision, expand to 2-5% only after proving profitability
- Use **Advantage+ Placements** initially, then optimise based on data
- Video ads on mobile outperform static images — hook viewers in the first 3 seconds
- Start with **Ad Set Budget** for testing, switch to **Campaign Budget Optimisation** for scaling
- A/B test one variable at a time — audiences, creatives, or copy — never everything at once
- Indian benchmarks: CPC ₹5-15, CTR 1-3%, CPL ₹100-300
- Scale winning campaigns gradually — increase budget by maximum 20-30% every 2-3 days
