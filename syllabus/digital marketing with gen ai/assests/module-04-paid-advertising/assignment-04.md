## Task 1: ROAS & Performance Analysis (25 Marks)

### Brief
You are a performance marketing analyst at a digital agency in Pune. Your manager has given you data from 5 campaigns and wants your recommendation on which to scale, optimize, or pause.

### Campaign Data

| Campaign | Platform | Spend (₹) | Impressions | Clicks | Conversions | Revenue (₹) |
|----------|----------|-----------|-------------|--------|-------------|-------------|
| A — Diwali Sale | Meta Ads | 50,000 | 500,000 | 8,000 | 120 | 3,60,000 |
| B — Brand Awareness | Meta Ads | 30,000 | 1,200,000 | 4,500 | 15 | 45,000 |
| C — Search — Course | Google Ads | 25,000 | 40,000 | 2,000 | 80 | 2,40,000 |
| D — Display Retargeting | Google Ads | 15,000 | 300,000 | 1,500 | 45 | 90,000 |
| E — YouTube Pre-roll | Google Ads | 20,000 | 800,000 | 3,200 | 10 | 30,000 |

### Requirements

**Part A: Calculate Metrics** (10 marks)

For each campaign, calculate:
- CPC (Cost per Click)
- CTR (Click-through Rate)
- CPM (Cost per 1000 Impressions)
- CPA (Cost per Acquisition/Conversion)
- ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)
- Conversion Rate

Present in a clean table format.

**Part B: Analysis & Recommendation** (10 marks)

For each campaign, write:
- **Verdict:** SCALE / OPTIMIZE / PAUSE
- **Reasoning:** 2-3 sentences explaining why (compare to industry benchmarks)
- **Specific action:** What would you do next? (e.g., "Increase budget by 30%," "Test new audiences," "Pause and reallocate budget to Campaign C")

**Part C: Budget Reallocation** (5 marks)

You have a total monthly budget of ₹1,40,000 (same as current total). Based on your analysis:
- How would you redistribute the budget across campaigns?
- Show your proposed budget split with percentage allocation
- Justify your reallocation in 100 words

### Marking Criteria:
- Calculation accuracy: 10 marks
- Quality of analysis and reasoning: 10 marks
- Budget reallocation logic: 5 marks

## Task 2: Meta Ads Campaign Structure (25 Marks)

### Brief
Create a complete Meta Ads campaign plan for TechPath Academy's new batch launch in Bhopal. Monthly budget: ₹20,000. Goal: Generate 100 student enquiries (leads).

### Requirements

**Part A: Campaign Setup** (8 marks)

Document:
- Campaign Objective (and why you chose it)
- Campaign budget and schedule (daily vs lifetime, duration)
- Campaign bid strategy
- Conversion event to optimize for

**Part B: Ad Sets (3 different audiences)** (9 marks)

Create 3 Ad Sets with different audience strategies:

**Ad Set 1 — Interest-based targeting:**
- Name for the ad set
- Age range, gender, location (with radius)
- Detailed targeting: list all interests, behaviors, and demographics selected
- Placement selection (automatic or manual — justify)
- Daily budget for this ad set

**Ad Set 2 — Lookalike audience:**
- Source audience (what Custom Audience would you create the Lookalike from?)
- Lookalike percentage (1%, 2%, 5% — justify your choice)
- Any additional targeting layered on top
- Daily budget

**Ad Set 3 — Retargeting:**
- Which Custom Audience are you retargeting? (website visitors, video viewers, page engagers)
- Time window (last 7, 14, 30, 60 days — justify)
- Exclusions (who to exclude)
- Daily budget

**Part C: Ad Creatives (2 per Ad Set = 6 total)** (8 marks)

For each Ad Set, create 2 ad creatives:
- Ad format (Image, Carousel, Video, Stories)
- Primary text (up to 125 characters)
- Headline (up to 40 characters)
- Description (up to 30 characters)
- Call-to-action button (Learn More, Sign Up, Apply Now, etc.)
- Visual concept description (what the image/video shows — describe it clearly)

### Marking Criteria:
- Campaign setup logic and justification: 8 marks
- Audience strategy (diversity, relevance, logic): 9 marks
- Creative quality and variety: 8 marks

## Task 3: Google Responsive Search Ads (25 Marks)

### Brief
Write complete RSA ad copy for TechPath Academy's "Digital Marketing Course" campaign targeting the keyword "digital marketing course in Bhopal."

### Requirements

**Part A: Write 15 Headlines** (12 marks)

Write 15 unique headlines (each max 30 characters). Must include:
- At least 3 headlines with the keyword "digital marketing"
- At least 2 headlines with proof/numbers (students placed, rating, years)
- At least 2 headlines with USP/differentiator
- At least 2 headlines with CTA (action words)
- At least 2 headlines with price/offer
- At least 2 headlines with brand name or location
- Remaining: creative variations

For each headline, note which category it falls into.

**Part B: Write 4 Descriptions** (8 marks)

Write 4 unique descriptions (each max 90 characters). Each must:
- Be a complete sentence that makes sense standalone
- Include relevant keywords naturally
- Cover different angles (curriculum, placement, credibility, offer)
- Include a CTA in at least 2 descriptions

**Part C: Pinning Strategy & Reasoning** (5 marks)

- Which headlines would you pin to Position 1? Why?
- Which headlines would you pin to Position 2? Why?
- Which headlines would you leave unpinned? Why?
- What's the risk of pinning too many headlines?
- Write the 3 best possible combinations (H1 + H2 + H3 + Description) that Google might show

### Marking Criteria:
- Headline quality and variety: 12 marks
- Description quality and keyword usage: 8 marks
- Pinning strategy and reasoning: 5 marks

## Task 4: YouTube Ad Storyboard (25 Marks)

### Brief
Create a complete storyboard for a 30-second YouTube skippable in-stream ad promoting TechPath Academy's Digital Marketing course. The ad will target students and recent graduates (18-25) in Madhya Pradesh.

### Requirements

**Part A: Ad Script** (10 marks)

Write a complete script with timing:

| Time | Visual | Audio/Voiceover | Text on Screen |
|------|--------|----------------|----------------|
| 0-5s | (HOOK — what viewer sees) | (What they hear) | (Any text overlay) |
| 5-10s | ... | ... | ... |
| 10-15s | ... | ... | ... |
| 15-20s | ... | ... | ... |
| 20-25s | ... | ... | ... |
| 25-30s | ... | ... | ... |

Rules:
- First 5 seconds MUST hook the viewer (they can skip after 5s)
- Use a question, bold statement, or relatable scenario as hook
- Include at least one testimonial/proof moment
- End with clear CTA (what to do next)
- Mention price or "starting from ₹X" if relevant
- Include TechPath branding (logo, colors)

**Part B: Shot List** (5 marks)

For each scene (6 scenes, 5 seconds each):
- Shot type (close-up, medium, wide, overhead, screen recording)
- Location (classroom, office, outdoor, student's room)
- Props/Elements needed
- Actors needed (if any)
- Estimated production difficulty (Easy/Medium/Hard)

**Part C: Thumbnail Design** (5 marks)

Design description for the video thumbnail (if it appears as an in-feed/discovery ad):
- What the thumbnail shows (describe the image)
- Text overlay on thumbnail (max 5 words)
- Color scheme
- Why this thumbnail would get clicks (psychological trigger)

**Part D: Campaign Targeting** (5 marks)

- Campaign objective for this YouTube ad
- Target audience definition (demographics + interests + in-market segments)
- Bidding strategy (CPV or Target CPM — justify)
- Daily budget recommendation
- Expected results (estimate views, view rate, CPC based on benchmarks)
- How you'll measure success (KPIs)

### Marking Criteria:
- Script quality (hook, story, CTA): 10 marks
- Shot list professionalism: 5 marks
- Thumbnail design thinking: 5 marks
- Campaign strategy: 5 marks
