## Why Google Ads?

Google Ads is the most powerful advertising platform because it captures **intent**. Unlike Meta Ads where you interrupt people scrolling through their feed, Google Ads shows your ad to people who are **actively searching** for what you offer.

> **Simple Analogy:** Meta Ads is like putting up a hoarding on a busy road — people see it but were not looking for you. Google Ads is like opening a shop right at the entrance of a market where customers are already walking in to buy — they are already looking for what you sell.

## Google Ads Campaign Types

| Campaign Type | Where Ads Appear | Best For | Targeting |
|--------------|-----------------|----------|-----------|
| **Search** | Google search results (text ads at top/bottom) | Capturing high-intent buyers | Keywords |
| **Display** | Banners on 2 million+ websites and apps | Brand awareness, retargeting | Audiences, topics, placements |
| **Shopping** | Product listings with image, price, store name | E-commerce products | Product feed |
| **Video (YouTube)** | YouTube videos (before, during, or alongside) | Brand storytelling, product demos | Topics, keywords, audiences |
| **Performance Max** | Across ALL Google channels (Search, Display, YouTube, Maps, Gmail, Discover) | Maximum conversions with automation | Audience signals + AI |
| **App** | Google Search, Play Store, YouTube, Display Network | App installs and engagement | Automated by Google |

> **For beginners:** Start with Search campaigns — they deliver the highest ROI because you are targeting people who are already searching for your product or service.

## Search Ads — Deep Dive

### Keyword Match Types

When you add keywords to your Search campaign, you choose how broadly or narrowly Google matches your keyword to user searches.

| Match Type | Symbol | How It Works | Example Keyword | Triggers For |
|-----------|--------|-------------|----------------|-------------|
| **Broad Match** | No symbol | Widest reach, matches related searches, synonyms, and variations | `digital marketing course` | "online marketing training", "DM classes near me", "learn social media marketing" |
| **Phrase Match** | "quotes" | Matches searches that include the meaning of your keyword in order | `"digital marketing course"` | "best digital marketing course in Bhopal", "digital marketing course fees" |
| **Exact Match** | [brackets] | Matches searches with the same meaning/intent as your keyword | `[digital marketing course bhopal]` | "digital marketing course bhopal", "DM course bhopal" |
| **Negative Keywords** | -minus | Blocks your ad from showing for irrelevant searches | `-free`, `-download`, `-PDF` | Prevents clicks from people searching "free digital marketing course" |

### Which Match Type to Use?

| Situation | Recommended Match Type |
|-----------|----------------------|
| New campaign, testing keywords | Phrase Match (balanced reach and relevance) |
| High-performing proven keywords | Exact Match (maximum control) |
| Expanding to discover new keywords | Broad Match (with negative keywords added) |
| Always | Add Negative Keywords to all campaigns |

> **Real Example:** A coaching institute added the broad match keyword `coaching` without negatives. They got clicks for "IPL cricket coaching", "dog training coaching", and "life coaching spiritual" — all completely irrelevant. Always add negative keywords like `-cricket`, `-dog`, `-spiritual`, `-free`, `-PDF`, `-download`.

## Writing RSAs (Responsive Search Ads)

**RSA** = Responsive Search Ad. Google's current standard search ad format. You provide up to **15 headlines** and **4 descriptions**, and Google's AI mixes and matches them to find the best combinations.

### RSA Structure

| Element | Limit | Rules |
|---------|-------|-------|
| **Headlines** | Up to 15 (minimum 3) | 30 characters each |
| **Descriptions** | Up to 4 (minimum 2) | 90 characters each |
| **Display URL Paths** | 2 paths | 15 characters each (e.g., /courses/digital-marketing) |

### Writing 15 Headlines — Strategy

| Category | Count | Examples |
|----------|-------|---------|
| **Keyword-focused** (include primary keyword) | 3-4 | "Digital Marketing Course Bhopal", "Learn Digital Marketing", "DM Training Institute" |
| **Benefit-focused** | 3-4 | "100% Placement Assistance", "Industry Expert Trainers", "Get Certified in 6 Months" |
| **Social proof** | 2-3 | "500+ Students Placed", "4.8 Star Rating", "Trusted Since 2020" |
| **CTA-focused** | 2-3 | "Enroll Now - Limited Seats", "Book Free Demo Class", "Download Brochure" |
| **Offer/Urgency** | 1-2 | "Early Bird: 20% Off", "New Batch Starting Aug 2026" |

### Writing 4 Descriptions

| # | Focus | Example |
|---|-------|---------|
| 1 | What you offer | "Comprehensive 6-month Digital Marketing course covering SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, and AI tools." |
| 2 | Why choose you | "Learn from industry practitioners with 10+ years experience. Real projects, not just theory." |
| 3 | Social proof + CTA | "Join 500+ placed students. Book your free demo class today. EMI options available." |
| 4 | Offer/USP | "Includes Google Ads & Meta certification prep. Placement support in 200+ companies across India." |

### Pinning

**Pinning** forces a specific headline to always appear in a specific position.

| Position | When to Pin |
|----------|-------------|
| Headline 1 | Pin your brand name or primary keyword headline here if needed for compliance |
| Headline 2 | Only pin if legally required (e.g., disclaimers) |
| Do not pin | Most of the time — let Google test all combinations for best performance |

> **Pro Tip:** Google shows 2-3 headlines in most search results. The more unique headlines you provide (all 15), the more combinations Google can test. Ads with all 15 headlines and 4 descriptions have higher Ad Strength and typically get 10-15% more clicks.

## Ad Extensions (Assets)

Ad extensions add extra information below your ad, making it larger and more clickable. They are free to add and improve your Quality Score.

| Extension | What It Adds | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Sitelinks** | Links to other pages on your site (4-6 links) | "SEO Course", "Web Dev Course", "Student Reviews", "Contact Us" |
| **Callouts** | Short benefit phrases (no links) | "Free Demo Class", "EMI Available", "Weekend Batches", "100% Placement" |
| **Structured Snippets** | Category-based list | "Courses: SEO, Google Ads, Social Media, Email Marketing" |
| **Price** | Price list for services | "Digital Marketing: ₹45,000", "Web Dev: ₹55,000" |
| **Call** | Phone number (clickable on mobile) | "+91-7000-123456" |
| **Location** | Business address from Google Business Profile | "MP Nagar Zone 2, Bhopal" |
| **Image** | Thumbnail image next to your ad | Your campus or student photo |
| **Lead Form** | In-ad form (no website needed) | Name, Email, Phone fields directly in the ad |

> **Pro Tip:** Always add at least Sitelinks, Callouts, and Structured Snippets to every campaign. They are free, increase your ad size by 15-20%, and directly improve your Quality Score.

## Display Network

The **Google Display Network (GDN)** reaches over 90% of internet users worldwide through banner ads on 2 million+ websites, apps, and YouTube.

### Banner Sizes (Most Important)

| Size | Name | Where It Appears |
|------|------|-----------------|
| 300 x 250 | Medium Rectangle | Inline within content (most common) |
| 728 x 90 | Leaderboard | Top of pages |
| 160 x 600 | Wide Skyscraper | Sidebar |
| 320 x 50 | Mobile Banner | Mobile websites |
| 300 x 600 | Half Page | Sidebar (high impact) |

### Responsive Display Ads

Instead of designing separate banners for each size, upload:
- Up to 15 images (landscape + square)
- Up to 5 logos
- Up to 5 headlines (30 chars each)
- Up to 5 descriptions (90 chars each)

Google automatically creates ads that fit any placement.

### Display Targeting Options

| Targeting | How It Works | Best For |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| **Placement** | Choose specific websites to show ads on | Showing ads on niche blogs your audience reads |
| **Topic** | Show ads on pages about certain topics | "Education", "Technology", "Business" |
| **In-Market** | People actively researching/shopping for something | "Education > Computer Training" |
| **Affinity** | People with long-term interests | "Technology Enthusiasts", "Business Professionals" |
| **Remarketing** | People who visited your website | Retargeting visitors who did not convert |

## Shopping Ads

For e-commerce businesses selling products online.

### Google Merchant Center Setup

1. Create a Merchant Center account (merchants.google.com)
2. Verify your website
3. Create a **Product Feed** (spreadsheet with all product details)
4. Upload the feed to Merchant Center
5. Link Merchant Center to Google Ads
6. Create a Shopping campaign

### Product Feed Requirements

| Field | Required | Example |
|-------|----------|---------|
| `id` | Yes | "SKU-12345" |
| `title` | Yes | "Blue Cotton Kurta for Men - Size L" |
| `description` | Yes | "Handwoven cotton kurta, comfortable for daily wear..." |
| `link` | Yes | "https://yoursite.com/products/blue-kurta" |
| `image_link` | Yes | "https://yoursite.com/images/blue-kurta.jpg" |
| `price` | Yes | "1,299.00 INR" |
| `availability` | Yes | "in_stock" |
| `brand` | Yes | "FabIndia" |
| `condition` | Yes | "new" |

## Performance Max Campaigns

**Performance Max (PMax)** is Google's AI-powered campaign type that runs across ALL Google channels simultaneously: Search, Display, YouTube, Gmail, Discover, and Maps.

### How It Works

1. You provide **asset groups** (images, videos, headlines, descriptions, logos)
2. You add **audience signals** (tell Google who your ideal customer is)
3. Google's AI creates ads and shows them across all channels
4. The AI optimises for your conversion goal automatically

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Maximum reach across all Google properties | Less control over where ads appear |
| Google's AI does the optimisation | Hard to see which channel drives results |
| Great for e-commerce (Shopping + Search + Display) | Can cannibalise your existing Search campaigns |
| Easy to set up (minimal manual work) | Requires good conversion tracking to work well |

> **Recommendation for beginners:** Start with Search campaigns first. Add Performance Max once you have strong conversion data (at least 30 conversions/month) for the AI to learn from.

## Bidding Strategies

| Strategy | Type | How It Works | Best For |
|----------|------|-------------|----------|
| **Manual CPC** | Manual | You set the max bid for each keyword | Full control, learning phase |
| **Enhanced CPC (ECPC)** | Semi-automated | Google adjusts your bid up/down based on conversion likelihood | Transitioning from manual to automated |
| **Maximize Clicks** | Automated | Google gets the most clicks within your budget | Driving traffic, new campaigns |
| **Maximize Conversions** | Automated | Google gets the most conversions within your budget | Lead generation, sales (needs conversion tracking) |
| **Target CPA** | Automated | Google targets a specific cost per conversion | When you know your ideal CPL (e.g., ₹200/lead) |
| **Target ROAS** | Automated | Google targets a specific return on ad spend | E-commerce with revenue tracking |

### Bidding Strategy Progression

```
New campaign → Manual CPC (learn the market)
      ↓
After 2 weeks → Enhanced CPC (let Google help)
      ↓
After 30+ conversions → Maximize Conversions or Target CPA
      ↓
After 100+ conversions → Target ROAS (for e-commerce)
```

> **Important Rule:** Automated bidding strategies (Target CPA, Maximize Conversions) need at least 30 conversions in the last 30 days to work well. If you switch to Target CPA with only 5 conversions of data, the AI will not have enough information and your costs will spike.

## Quality Score Optimisation Tips

| Factor | Optimisation Tactic |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Expected CTR** | Include keyword in Headline 1, use numbers and power words, add all extensions |
| **Ad Relevance** | Create tight ad groups (5-10 closely related keywords), write ad copy that matches keywords exactly |
| **Landing Page** | Dedicated landing page per ad group (not homepage), include keyword on page, load under 3 seconds, mobile-friendly, clear CTA above the fold |

> **Pro Tip:** A Quality Score of 7+ should be your minimum target. Every point below 7 means you are paying more per click than necessary. Every point above 7 means you are getting a discount.

## Summary

- **Google Ads** captures intent — people are already searching for what you offer, making it the highest-ROI ad platform
- Six campaign types: Search (text), Display (banners), Shopping (products), Video (YouTube), Performance Max (all channels), App
- **Keyword match types**: Broad (widest reach), Phrase (balanced), Exact (most precise) — always add Negative Keywords
- **RSAs** take up to 15 headlines (30 chars) and 4 descriptions (90 chars) — Google AI mixes and matches the best combinations
- Add **ad extensions** (Sitelinks, Callouts, Structured Snippets, Price, Call) to every campaign — they are free and improve Quality Score
- **Display Network** reaches 90% of internet users through banner ads on 2 million+ websites
- **Performance Max** is Google's AI campaign type across all channels — use only after you have 30+ monthly conversions
- **Bidding progression**: Start Manual CPC, then Enhanced CPC, then Maximize Conversions / Target CPA after gathering conversion data
- **Quality Score** (7+ target) is the key to lower CPCs and better positions — optimise expected CTR, ad relevance, and landing page experience
